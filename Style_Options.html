Research notes

Liquid Glass, as Apple describes it, is a material for controls and navigation that float above content. It is not a fill for every card. The regular variant blurs and lifts the background so text stays readable. The clear variant is for pieces that sit on photos or video, and it needs a dimming layer when the picture is bright. Typography in the same system is larger, bolder, and left aligned. Thin weights are avoided. On iPhone, body text defaults to 17px and should not go below 11px.

Liquid Glass Design separates that reactive glass from older glassmorphism, which is a still recipe of blur, a hairline, and a shadow. This page stays in CSS, so the highlight is a top inset edge rather than a lens that bends the video. In round 1 the buttons stay the site’s Explore solutions chip. In round 2 each option has one button color of its own, and the menu’s accent is water blue instead of the red used in the reference.

The round 2 menu follows Liquid Glass SAAS Dropdown Component by Pranjay, a Dribbble shot from September 2026, as described in Liquid Glass website examples. On a flat light ground the panel has nothing colorful to blur, so the edge, the inner highlight, and the shadow do the work. The hovered row is a lighter pill, and the last action sits under a divider. There are no accounts on this site, so the rows are Solutions, Learn, Undergraduate Studies, and Ways to Help, with Donate and About under the line.
