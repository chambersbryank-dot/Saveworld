GOSAT-GW "IBUKI GW" (JAXA)

JAXA's IBUKI GW tracks carbon dioxide, methane and nitrogen dioxide while also measuring the water cycle.

Image: NASA/JPL-Caltech.

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • What is it? A satellite carrying TANSO-3 for A gas, like carbon dioxide or methane, that traps heat in the air around Earth, the way a greenhouse keeps plants warm. Glossary and AMSR3 for the water cycle.
  • Why does it matter? Countries need independent, consistent measurements of emissions to check climate pledges.

The Five W's of GOSAT-GW "IBUKI GW"

Who

JAXA with Japan's Ministry of the Environment and the National Institute for Environmental Studies.

Source: JAXA, IBUKI GW (GOSAT-GW) status

What

A satellite carrying TANSO-3 for greenhouse gases and AMSR3 for the water cycle.

Source: JAXA, IBUKI GW project page

When

Launched June 29, 2025 from Tanegashima Space Center.

Source: JAXA, IBUKI GW (GOSAT-GW) status

Where

Earth The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary, observing the whole globe.

Source: JAXA, IBUKI GW project page

Why

Countries need independent, consistent measurements of emissions to check climate pledges.

Source: JAXA, IBUKI GW project page

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

JAXA's IBUKI GW launched on June 29, 2025 on H-IIA No. 50. Its TANSO-3 instrument measures the greenhouse gases A gas that people and animals breathe out and that burning fuel releases. Plants take it in, and too much of it in the air warms the planet. Glossary and A greenhouse gas that traps much more heat than carbon dioxide over a few decades. It leaks from oil and gas sites, landfills and farms. Glossary, plus nitrogen dioxide, while AMSR3 observes the water cycle, putting greenhouse-gas and water-cycle monitoring on one satellite.

Source: JAXA, IBUKI GW (GOSAT-GW) status

Source: JAXA, IBUKI GW project page
