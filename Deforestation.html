Forests

Impact Statement

Forests come back when people plant and protect them. Life Terra reports more than 36 million trees planted with partners.

Organization source: Life Terra via Euractiv PR

Terraformation restores native, biodiverse forests from seed, and Saving Planets' Three Trees rule replaces every tree removed with three.

The Five W's of Forest Solutions

Who

Life Terra (nonprofit), Terraformation (company, with its nonprofit foundation) and Saving Planets' Three Trees rule.

What

Planting and tracking trees, and restoring native forests.

When

Ongoing.

Where

Europe (Life Terra), Hawaii and projects worldwide (Terraformation), and anywhere a tree is removed (Three Trees).

Why

Forests hold soil, shade land and give habitat. Replanting more than is lost rebuilds them.

Missions and Solutions

Great Green Wall (African Union / UNCCD)

An African-led effort to restore land across the Sahel; a 2020 UN status report counted about 4 million hectares inside its zone and nearly 18 million in the wider region (UNCCD).

Justdiggit

With farmers and pastoralists in Africa, Justdiggit is restoring 525,000 hectares of dry land and has brought back 32.3 million trees (organization figures).

One Tree Planted

Since 2014, One Tree Planted has planted more than 171 million trees with 489 partners in 84 countries (organization figure).

Rainforest Connection

Solar-powered Guardian listening devices and AI detect chainsaws in real time; Rainforest Connection reports more than 726,000 hectares of rainforest protected (organization figure).

Global Forest Watch (WRI)

Free satellite forest data and deforestation alerts from WRI; where Indigenous forest monitors used it, deforestation fell 52% in one year (Global Forest Watch).

Photo: SI Partner.

Active🌳 Forests

Life Terra

Life Terra and its partners have planted more than 36 million trees across Europe (company figure), working toward a goal of 500 million, with citizen engagement and tree monitoring technology.

Active🌳 Forests

Terraformation

Terraformation restores native, biodiverse forests from seed to canopy across the tropics and Hawaii.

Photo: SI Partner.

Active🌳 Forests

Three Trees for Every One Removed

For every tree removed, three go back in — rebuilding habitat, soil, and shade. A forest solution on Saving Planets.

Active🌳 Forests

Mangrove Forests

About 80 species of mangrove trees guard tropical coastlines, holding mud in place and softening storm surges.

Active🌳 Forests

Saharan Dust Feeds the Amazon

Every year about 27.7 million tons of Saharan dust falls on the Amazon, delivering some 22,000 tons of phosphorus that the rainforest needs (NASA).

Active🌳 Forests

The Amazon Makes Its Own Rainy Season

Water breathed out by Amazon trees starts the rainy season two to three months before winds alone would bring it (NASA JPL).

Active🌳 Forests

Trees for the Future

Partnering with East African farmers on regenerative farms: 450M+ trees grown and 99,000 acres restored (nonprofit figures).
