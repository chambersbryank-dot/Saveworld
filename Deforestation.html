Forest solutions

Three trees for every one removed

For every tree that comes out, three go in. That replaces what was lost and starts to rebuild habitat, soil, and shade. This is the forest solution on Saving Planets.

Terraformation

A Hawaii-based company restoring native, biodiverse forests from seed to canopy, with up to five times more native species than typical carbon projects. Their nonprofit arm, Terraformation.org Foundation, funds projects across Africa, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific, and individuals can fund a native tree in Hawaii for $25.

Read the full Terraformation page

Life Terra

One of Europe's largest climate-action initiatives, active in more than 30 countries. They have planted over 36 million trees with 125,000-plus participants, using monitoring technology so every tree is registered and tracked for at least 40 years.
