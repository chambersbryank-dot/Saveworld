Pendulum Period Calculator (Small and Large Angles)

Share to Google Classroom(opens in a new tab)

A pendulum’s period is the time for one full swing there and back. For small swings it depends only on length and gravity; for big swings it gets longer, and this calculator gives the exact value.

Standards:

  • AP1 Unit 7
  • IB C.1
  • CCSS HSA-CED.A.4

Our alignment; not reviewed by the standards bodies.

Formulas verified in build: 2026-10-16

Worked example

Given
  • Length L (m): 1 m
  • Amplitude θ₀ (°, below 180): 60 °
Answer
  • Small-angle period T₀: 2.0064 s
  • Exact period T: 2.1532 s
  • Longer than small-angle by: 7.32 %
Check
A 1 m pendulum has T₀ = 2.006 s, and at 60° the exact period is 1.0732 times longer, matching elliptic-integral tables; the build checks both.

Assumptions

Simple pendulum: point mass, light rigid string, no friction, standard gravity 9.80665 m/s².
