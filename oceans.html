Cleaning the seas, one solution at a time.

The Five W's of Ocean Cleanup

Who

The Ocean Cleanup, a nonprofit founded by Boyan Slat. Engineers, scientists, sailors, and volunteers working across continents to remove plastic from oceans and rivers.

What

They remove legacy plastic from garbage patches and coasts, intercept plastic flowing from rivers before it reaches the sea, and advocate for stronger international regulations like a Global Plastics Treaty.

When

Active for over ten years, with operations ongoing. Major milestones include passing fifteen million kilograms removed in July twenty twenty-four and breaking the ten million kilogram barrier in April twenty twenty-four.

Where

Globally: the Great Pacific Garbage Patch, coastal ecosystems, and around one thousand rivers worldwide that contribute eighty percent of riverine plastic pollution — including Indonesia, Thailand, Guatemala, Jamaica, Panama, Malaysia, and Los Angeles County.

Why

Plastic pollution is a worsening crisis. Trillions of pieces harm marine wildlife, degrade into microplastics that persist for decades, and enter the human food chain. Cleanup restores ecosystems and protects communities that depend on healthy seas.

Impact Statement

Their mission: clean up ninety percent of floating ocean plastic pollution by twenty forty. Tens of millions of kilograms of plastic have already been removed. Offshore systems like System 002 have offloaded up to fifty-five metric tons in a single mission, and Interceptor units have caught over two million kilograms at individual river sites.

Every kilogram pulled from the water is a kilogram that never breaks into microplastics, never entangles a sea turtle, and never reaches someone's dinner plate. The technology exists. The will is what turns cleanup into a habit.

The Ocean Cleanup

The Ocean Cleanup technologies are deployed around the world as they conduct the largest cleanup in history. For over ten years, they have been researching, extracting, and monitoring plastic pollution across oceans and rivers.
