EarthCARE (ESA–JAXA)

ESA–JAXA EarthCARE carries the first space cloud radar that can measure how cloud particles move up and down.

Image: NASA.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 2 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • What is it? A satellite with four instruments, including JAXA's Doppler Cloud Profiling A tool that sends out radio waves and listens for the echoes. From space it can see the ground through clouds, day or night. Glossary.
  • Why does it matter? Knowing whether clouds warm or cool the planet sharpens climate forecasts.

The Five W's of EarthCARE

Who

ESA and JAXA; JAXA and NICT built the Cloud Profiling Radar.

Source: ESA, EarthCARE

Source: JAXA, EarthCARE Cloud Profiling Radar

What

A satellite with four instruments, including JAXA's Doppler Cloud Profiling Radar.

Source: ESA, EarthCARE

When

Launched May 29, 2024 from Vandenberg, California on a Falcon 9.

Source: ESA, EarthCARE

Where

Low Earth The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary, profiling the atmosphere beneath it.

Source: ESA, EarthCARE

Why

Knowing whether clouds warm or cool the planet sharpens climate forecasts.

Source: ESA, EarthCARE

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

Clouds and Tiny bits of dust, smoke, salt or pollution floating in the air. They can block sunlight and help clouds form. Glossary are among the hardest parts of the climate to measure. EarthCARE launched on May 29, 2024 with four instruments that measure them together. Its JAXA Cloud Profiling Radar is the first spaceborne cloud radar with Doppler capability, measuring the vertical motion of cloud particles from orbit.

Source: ESA, EarthCARE

Source: JAXA, EarthCARE Cloud Profiling Radar
