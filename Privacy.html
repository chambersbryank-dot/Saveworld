Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how you stay in control. Short version: very little, and nothing without your OK.

NASA
  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The Short Version

Saving Planets has no accounts, no ads and no ad tracking. We do not sell or share personal information.

Analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage and does not build a profile of you or follow you across sites. We see only totals such as page views, referring sites, countries and browser types.

We stopped using Google Analytics on October 16, 2026, so there is no consent banner: nothing on this site sets a tracking cookie.

We also count a few actions as plain totals, using Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine: which learning lab was used, Learn page views, practice answers checked and correct, SI Helper questions by outcome (answered, no answer, refused; never the question text), newsletter sign-ups, share-button clicks (Google Classroom, copy link or copy embed code), which calculator was used and clicks to partner organizations on Ways to Help. Each count stores only the action, the page language and a fixed label, with no IP address, no cookie and no identifier. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted. The Google Classroom button is a plain link: nothing is sent to Google unless you click it.

Newsletter (MailerLite)

If you subscribe to the Weekly Saving Planets Brief, your email address and chosen language are stored by our email provider, MailerLite, only to send the newsletter. You confirm by email first (double opt-in), every issue has an unsubscribe link, and the sign-up form sets no cookies.

Children and Students

Kids pages (Kids Planet Map, Amazing Facts, Satellite Explorer) ask for no personal information and have no chat, comments or uploads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

The newsletter is meant for parents, teachers and adults. If you believe a child sent us an email address, write to us and we will delete it.

Analytics on every page, including kids pages, is cookie-free and counts totals only. Lesson plans are built in your browser and are not sent to us.

Stored in Your Browser

We save small settings, such as badges and progress in a learning tool, in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device.

Hosting and Contact

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security. Questions or deletion requests: privacy@savingplanets.com.

The Report a correction form sends only the page address, what is wrong and your optional suggested fix to our inbox (media@savingplanets.com) by email through Cloudflare. It has no name or email fields and refuses messages that contain an email address or phone number. Nothing is stored on the site; an invisible Cloudflare Turnstile check and short-lived rate limits stop spam.

SI Helper (pilot, Teachers page)

Added October 16, 2026. The Teachers page has an optional question box, SI Helper, run on Cloudflare (Workers AI). Question and answer text is not stored or logged, and SI Helper sets no cookies. To apply rate limits it keeps only same-day counters based on a salted, one-way code of your IP address, erased daily. An invisible Cloudflare Turnstile check loads only when you click in the box.
