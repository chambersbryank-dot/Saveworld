Credit (opt-in only): name/handle exactly as given, or "an anonymous reader"; children first name + country only; no photos; never publish emails. Credit group: Language Partners.
Targets: acknowledge within 2 business days; fix in the generator (never by hand), test on a branch, deploy; 2 days for meaning/safety, 7 days for wording. Decline changes that alter the English meaning or cited facts.
