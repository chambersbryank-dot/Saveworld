Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

NASA's PUNCH (Polarimeter to Unify the Corona and Heliosphere) is a constellation of four small satellites in low Earth orbit, launched March 11, 2025. Together they make global, 3D observations of the Sun's corona to learn how its mass and energy become the solar wind that fills the solar system, treating the Sun, solar wind and Earth as one connected system.

Source: NASA, PUNCH

Better understanding of the solar wind improves the space weather forecasts that protect satellites and power grids.
