Coral Gardeners

Coral Gardeners grows corals in nurseries and replants them on reefs in French Polynesia, Fiji and Thailand: 238,250 corals planted so far (organization figures).

Image: NASA (STS093-717-066, coral reefs of Bora Bora, French Polynesia).

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Coral reefs are like busy underwater cities for fish, and many are getting sick from hot water.
  • Coral Gardeners grow baby corals in underwater nurseries, like a garden.
  • When the corals are big enough, divers plant them back on the reef so it can grow again.

The Five W's of Coral Gardeners

Who

Coral Gardeners, a reef restoration group, working with local "gardeners" at each branch.

Source: Coral Gardeners, Our impact

What

Growing coral fragments in nurseries and replanting them on damaged reefs, using new tools and methods.

Source: Coral Gardeners, Our impact

When

It has planted 238,250 corals to date and runs 12 active restoration sites.

Source: Coral Gardeners, Our impact

Where

French Polynesia, Fiji and Thailand.

Source: Coral Gardeners, Our impact

Why

Reefs shelter fish and protect coasts, but many are being damaged and need help to recover.

Source: Coral Gardeners, Our impact

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

Coral Gardeners reports 238,250 corals planted, more than 80 jobs created and 25,100 square meters of reef restored (organization figures).

Source: Coral Gardeners, Our impact

For Further Information: Coral Gardeners, Our impact

Coral Gardeners has replanted 238,250 corals across 12 reef sites in three countries.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

The work has created more than 80 jobs (organization figures), and healthy reefs protect coasts and support fishing and tourism.

Source: Coral Gardeners, Our impact

For Nature

Replanted corals rebuild living reef, about 25,100 square meters so far (organization figures), giving fish and other sea life a home again.

Source: Coral Gardeners, Our impact
