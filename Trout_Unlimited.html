Trout Unlimited

Trout Unlimited restores and reconnects cold, clean rivers, reconnecting 1,052 miles of streams in its 2024-25 season (Trout Unlimited).

Image: NASA (PIA26448).

  1. Home
  2. Rivers
  3. Trout Unlimited

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 3 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Trout and salmon need cold, clean rivers to live.
  • Trout Unlimited fixes damaged streams and removes things that block fish.
  • More than 20,000 volunteers help care for rivers.

The Five W's of Trout Unlimited

Who

Trout Unlimited, a U.S. nonprofit with more than 400 local chapters and councils (organization figure).

Source: Trout Unlimited, 2024-25 Annual Report

What

It protects headwaters, reconnects broken habitat, restores All the land where rain and snow drain into the same river or lake. Glossary and gets communities involved.

Source: Trout Unlimited

When

In its 2024-25 season, volunteers gave 645,051 hours to conservation and science projects (organization figure).

Source: Trout Unlimited, 2024-25 Annual Report

Where

Rivers and streams across the United States, including more than $5 million invested in 20 projects in the Upper Bear River Watershed.

Source: Trout Unlimited

Why

Cold, clean water is vital for trout and salmon, and healthy rivers benefit the communities around them.

Source: Trout Unlimited

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

In 2024-25, Trout Unlimited restored 539 miles of rivers and streams and reconnected 1,052 miles (organization figures).

Source: Trout Unlimited, 2024-25 Annual Report

For Further Information: Trout Unlimited, 2024-25 Annual Report

Trout Unlimited received the first EPA permit to clean up an abandoned mine under the new Good Samaritan program.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

More than 20,000 volunteers take part in Trout Unlimited projects (organization figure).

Source: Trout Unlimited, 2024-25 Annual Report

For Nature

Reconnecting streams lets fish reach the cold water and spawning habitat they need.

Source: Trout Unlimited, 2024-25 Annual Report
