Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast (NOAA)

NOAA forecasts and tracks toxic algae in western Lake Erie through the summer so cities and swimmers can keep drinking water and people safe (NOAA NCCOS).

Image: NASA Goddard (Great Lakes, No Clouds, August 28, 2010).

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 4 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Sometimes tiny green living things called algae grow so much in Lake Erie that the water turns green.
  • Some of this algae makes poison that can get into drinking water.
  • NOAA watches the lake from space and from boats and warns people where the bad water is, like a weather report.

The Five W's of Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecast (NOAA)

Who

NOAA's National Centers for Coastal Ocean Science and its Great Lakes Environmental Research Laboratory, with university partners.

Source: NOAA NCCOS, Western Lake Erie HAB Seasonal Forecast 2026

What

A seasonal forecast plus regular bulletins that show where the cyanobacteria When tiny water plants called algae grow out of control. Some make toxins that can harm people, pets and fish. Glossary is and where it is moving.

Source: NOAA NCCOS, Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecasting

When

Every bloom season; the 2026 seasonal forecast came out June 25 and predicted a moderate bloom.

Source: NOAA NCCOS, Western Lake Erie HAB Seasonal Forecast 2026

Where

The western basin of Lake Erie, including Maumee Bay near Toledo, Ohio.

Source: NOAA NCCOS, Western Lake Erie HAB Seasonal Forecast 2026

Why

Phosphorus washing in from the Maumee River feeds blooms whose toxins can reach drinking water.

Source: NOAA NCCOS, Western Lake Erie HAB Seasonal Forecast 2026

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

In August 2014 a bloom put toxins in Toledo's tap water and the city told 400,000 people not to drink it for two days. NOAA shared fast toxin data with the city and Ohio EPA to help keep drinking water safe.

Source: NOAA NCCOS, Timely toxin data helped Ohio deliver safe drinking water

For Further Information: NOAA NCCOS, Lake Erie Harmful Algal Bloom Forecasting

NOAA forecasts toxic algae on Lake Erie the way meteorologists forecast storms.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Bulletins warn water plants and families where scum is, so people and pets can stay out of toxic water.

Source: NOAA NCCOS, Western Lake Erie HAB Seasonal Forecast 2026

For Nature

Beyond Lake Erie, the EPA's CyAN tool uses satellite data to flag possible algal blooms in more than 2,000 large U.S. lakes and reservoirs.

Source: US EPA, CyAN app
