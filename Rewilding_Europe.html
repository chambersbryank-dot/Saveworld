Rewilding Europe

Rewilding Europe helps wild nature come back across Europe. Wild European bison grew from about 2,500 to 7,000 in 10 years, helped by its work (organization figures).

Image: NASA (Danube Delta, where the Danube meets the Black Sea).

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Rewilding means letting nature take care of itself again.

The Five W's of Rewilding Europe

Who

Rewilding Europe, working with local partners in large pioneering landscapes.

Source: Rewilding Europe

What

Rewilding: bringing back wildlife and natural processes such as grazing and free-flowing rivers, and supporting nature-based businesses.

Source: Rewilding Europe

When

Over the last 10 years, wild European bison increased from about 2,500 to 7,000, with Rewilding Europe's efforts contributing.

Source: Rewilding Europe

Where

Rewilding landscapes across Europe, including the Southern Carpathians in Romania and the Central Apennines in Italy.

Source: Rewilding Europe

Why

The variety of all living things in a place: plants, animals, fungi and tiny microbes. Glossary loss and climate change are getting worse, and rewilding is one of the most direct ways to reverse both.

Source: Rewilding Europe

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

Rewilding Europe reports that wild European bison numbers rose from 2,500 to 7,000 over 10 years, with its efforts contributing, and that vulture populations are slowly recovering (organization figures).

Source: Rewilding Europe

For Further Information: Rewilding Europe

Wild European bison, Europe's largest land mammal, grew from about 2,500 to 7,000 in 10 years.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Its Wilder Places platform lets visitors book nature trips that directly support local communities.

Source: Rewilding Europe

For Nature

Returning grazers, vultures and healthier rivers restore natural processes and help wildlife come back.

Source: Rewilding Europe
