Kelp Forests

Kelp can grow up to 18 inches a day, building underwater forests that feed and shelter thousands of species.

Image: NASA (ISS018-E-018110).

  • What is it? Underwater forests of giant algae.

The Five W's of Kelp Forests

Who

Nature, studied and protected by NOAA and partners.

Source: NOAA, Kelp forests

What

Underwater forests of giant algae.

Source: NOAA, Kelp forests

When

Year-round, growing up to 18 inches a day in ideal conditions.

Source: NOAA, Kelp forests

Where

Cool, nutrient-rich coastal waters.

Source: NOAA, Kelp forests

Why

They feed and shelter thousands of species.

Source: NOAA, Kelp forests

Impact Statement

Natural solution · active

In ideal conditions, Large brown seaweed that grows in cool ocean water and forms underwater forests full of sea life. Glossary can grow up to 18 inches per day. Like trees in a forest, giant kelp canopies provide food and shelter for thousands of species, from fish and invertebrates to seals, sea lions, whales, sea otters and seabirds, NOAA says.

Source: NOAA, What is a kelp forest?

For Further Information: NOAA, What is a kelp forest?

In ideal conditions, kelp can grow up to 18 inches in a single day.
