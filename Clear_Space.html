ClearSpace

Europe’s debris-removal mission: capture a dead object and bring it down.

The Five W's of ClearSpace

Who

ClearSpace, a Swiss company founded in 2018 as a spin-off from EPFL. Headquarters are in Luxembourg, with offices in Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and Germany.

What

A servicer that finds a non-responsive object, captures it, and steers it into a destructive reentry. ClearSpace-1 has been described as a four-armed capture spacecraft. PRELUDE is a separate close-proximity test mission.

When

ESA signed the ClearSpace-1 contract in November 2020, worth 86 million euros. The original target was the VESPA adapter from a 2013 Vega flight. In April 2024 the target changed to the PROBA-1 satellite. Wikipedia lists a planned 2028 launch. PRELUDE was announced with ESA on 12 January 2026 and has not flown.

Where

Low Earth orbit. ClearSpace-1 is an ESA Space Safety mission, planned to launch on Vega-C from the Guiana Space Centre.

Why

A dead satellite or rocket part can break into many pieces. Removing one large object before it fragments cuts collision risk for everything else in that orbit.

Impact Statement

ClearSpace-1 is still a planned mission, not a completed removal. The target change from VESPA to PROBA-1 and the 2028 launch date are the current public figures. PRELUDE is a technology test announced in 2026, not an operational cleanup.
