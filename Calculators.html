Aerospace & Physics Calculators

Share to Google Classroom(opens in a new tab)

Type your numbers, see the answer and the equation behind it. Every formula is checked against textbook values.

Spaceflight and orbits

  • Delta-v Calculator (Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation)Delta-v is how much a rocket can change its speed by burning its propellant.
  • Hohmann Transfer CalculatorA Hohmann transfer moves a spacecraft between two circular orbits with two engine burns.
  • Orbital Period CalculatorThe orbital period is how long a satellite takes to go once around the Earth.
  • Orbital Velocity & Escape Velocity CalculatorOrbital velocity is the speed needed to stay in a circular orbit at a given height.
  • Thrust-to-Weight Ratio CalculatorThe thrust-to-weight ratio compares an engine’s push with the vehicle’s weight.

Flight and aerodynamics

  • Stall Speed Calculator (with Bank Angle and Altitude)Stall speed is the slowest a wing can fly while still holding the aircraft up.
  • Lift Equation CalculatorThe lift equation says how much upward force a wing makes.
  • Mach Number & Speed of Sound Calculator (Standard Atmosphere)The speed of sound depends only on air temperature, so it falls as you climb until about 11 km, then stays the same.
  • Rotor Hover Power Calculator (Momentum Theory)A hovering helicopter or drone pushes air down to hold itself up.

Physics

  • Projectile Motion Calculator with Air ResistanceReal balls do not follow a perfect parabola: air drag shortens the range and lowers the top.
  • Pendulum Period Calculator (Small and Large Angles)A pendulum’s period is the time for one full swing there and back.
