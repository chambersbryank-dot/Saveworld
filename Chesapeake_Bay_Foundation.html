Chesapeake Bay Foundation

Since 1966, the Chesapeake Bay Foundation has worked to save the Bay and its rivers, adding more than 21 million oysters in fiscal year 2026 (CBF).

Image: NASA.

  1. Home
  2. Rivers
  3. Chesapeake Bay Foundation

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 4 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • The Chesapeake Bay is a huge bay fed by many rivers.
  • Oysters clean the water, so this group grows and plants millions of them.
  • They also plant trees by streams and teach students outdoors.

The Five W's of Chesapeake Bay Foundation

Who

The Chesapeake Bay Foundation, founded in 1966, the largest independent conservation organization dedicated solely to saving the Bay.

Source: Chesapeake Bay Foundation, About

What

Oyster restoration, streamside tree planting, education and advocacy for healthy rivers and streams.

Source: Chesapeake Bay Foundation, FY2026 Impact Report

When

Over more than 50 years, including hands-on education programs for students and teachers.

Source: Chesapeake Bay Foundation

Where

The Chesapeake Bay and its All the land where rain and snow drain into the same river or lake. Glossary, home to more than 3,600 species of plants and animals.

Source: Chesapeake Bay Foundation

Why

A healthy Bay supports wildlife and people. The Bay recently earned a C+, the highest grade in 40 years of report cards from the University of Maryland Center for Environmental Science.

Source: Chesapeake Bay Foundation

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

In fiscal year 2026, CBF added more than 21 million oysters to the Bay (organization figure).

Source: Chesapeake Bay Foundation, FY2026 Impact Report

For Further Information: Chesapeake Bay Foundation, About

The Chesapeake Bay is home to more than 3,600 species of plants and animals.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

CBF's education programs have connected students, teachers and school leaders to the watershed for more than 50 years.

Source: Chesapeake Bay Foundation

For Nature

Oyster reefs and streamside forests improve water quality and give wildlife a home (CBF).

Source: Chesapeake Bay Foundation, FY2026 Impact Report
