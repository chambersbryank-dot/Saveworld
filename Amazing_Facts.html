Amazing Facts

Mind-blowing numbers behind the movement to save our planet.

Facts That Matter

Fact 1: Petabytes in Orbit

Starcloud's roadmap scales from a single sixty-kilogram satellite carrying one NVIDIA H100 GPU to Starcloud-3, a three-ton spacecraft generating about two hundred kilowatts — enough to store and process petabytes of data in space, powered entirely by the sun with zero water cooling. Google's Project Suncatcher launched October first, twenty twenty-six with four AI chips aboard, proving orbital compute is real and growing fast.

Fact 2: Twelve Times the Water

U.S. data centers directly consume about seventeen point four billion gallons of water a year, but the power plants generating their electricity consume roughly two hundred eleven billion gallons — more than twelve times the on-site amount. Every gigabyte streamed has a hidden water footprint most people never see.

Fact 3: Fifteen Million Kilograms

The Ocean Cleanup removed fifteen million kilograms of plastic from the world's oceans by July twenty twenty-four, breaking ten million kilograms just three months earlier. Their offshore systems have offloaded up to fifty-five metric tons in a single extraction from the Great Pacific Garbage Patch.

Fact 4: One Thousand Rivers

Around one thousand rivers worldwide contribute eighty percent of riverine plastic pollution reaching the ocean. The Ocean Cleanup's River Interceptors have caught over two million kilograms in Guatemala alone and more than one and a half million in Thailand.

Fact 5: Three Trees for One

For every tree cut down, planting three replaces it and then some — restoring habitat, pulling carbon from the air, and rebuilding soil. Reforestation projects on this site follow exactly that ratio.

Why Facts Matter

Numbers turn hope into action. When you can see that fifteen million kilograms of plastic is already out of the water, or that a single orbital satellite can out-compute anything previously flown in space, the future stops feeling abstract. These facts are the receipts behind every solution on this site.
