Amazing Facts That Matter

Numbers behind the work. Sources belong on each fact.

Facts That Matter

One Thousand Rivers

About 1,000 rivers contribute most riverine plastic reaching the ocean, per a Science Advances riverine-plastic study.

Room For 1,300 Earths

You could fit roughly 1,300 Earths inside Jupiter (about 1,321 by volume).

From: Jupiter: Earth's Natural Debris Shield

Source: NASA Jupiter Fact Sheet

Sixty Million Kilograms

The Ocean Cleanup reported 60 million kilograms of plastic removed as of July 2026 — their biggest year ever was 2025, with 25 million kilograms alone.

Three Trees For One

The site target: for every tree removed, three go in.

Zero Gallons In Orbit

Orbital compute radiates heat into space and does not use water for cooling — while U.S. data centers use about 17 billion gallons of water yearly.
