Amazing Facts

Mind-blowing numbers behind the movement to save our planet.

Facts That Matter

Fact 1: Petabytes in Orbit

Starcloud's roadmap scales from a single 60-kilogram satellite carrying one NVIDIA H100 GPU to Starcloud-3, a three-ton spacecraft generating about 200 kilowatts — enough to store and process petabytes of data in space, powered entirely by the sun with zero water cooling.

Fact 2: Twelve Times the Water

U.S. data centers directly consume about 17.4 billion gallons of water a year, but the power plants generating their electricity consume roughly 211 billion gallons — more than twelve times the on-site amount.

Fact 3: Fifteen Million Kilograms

The Ocean Cleanup removed 15 million kilograms of plastic from the world's oceans by July 2024. Their offshore systems have offloaded up to 55 metric tons in a single extraction from the Great Pacific Garbage Patch.

Fact 4: One Thousand Rivers

Around 1,000 rivers worldwide contribute 80 percent of riverine plastic pollution reaching the ocean. River Interceptors have caught over 2 million kilograms in Guatemala alone and more than 1.5 million in Thailand.

Fact 5: Three Trees for One

For every tree cut down, planting three replaces it and then some — restoring habitat, pulling carbon from the air, and rebuilding soil.

Why Facts Matter

Numbers turn hope into action. These facts are the receipts behind every solution on this site.
