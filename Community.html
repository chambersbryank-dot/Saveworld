Show Us Your Cleanup

Picked up trash on a beach, planted a tree, or joined a citizen-science project? Send us a photo and we may feature it here, so others can see real people doing the work.

Email your photo

Email ideas@savingplanets.com with the subject Community photo.

Guidelines

  • Send photos you took yourself. Please don't send photos from the internet or someone else's camera.
  • Use your first name or an alias only. We never publish last names, emails or addresses.
  • No faces of children or teens. Hands, gear, bags of trash and the beach are perfect.
  • Tell us where and when (city or beach is enough) and what you did.
  • By sending, you agree that Saving Planets may publish your photo, name or alias and caption on this site and our social accounts. Ask us any time and we'll take it down.
  • We read every email, but we may decline a photo or edit a caption for length or clarity.
