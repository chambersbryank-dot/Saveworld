Copernicus Sentinel-4 (ESA / EUMETSAT)

From geostationary orbit on MTG-S1, Sentinel-4 measures air pollution over Europe and North Africa every hour (ESA).

Image: NASA (iss071e207494).

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 2 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Sentinel-4 is a space tool that watches the air over Europe.
  • It stays above the same spot and checks the air every hour.
  • This helps warn people when the air is dirty.

The Five W's of Copernicus Sentinel-4 (ESA / EUMETSAT)

Who

ESA built it for the EU's Copernicus program; EUMETSAT operates the MTG-S1 satellite that carries it.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-4

What

An ultraviolet, visible and near-infrared spectrometer that measures pollutants such as nitrogen dioxide and A gas high in the sky that blocks harmful ultraviolet rays from the Sun. Near the ground, ozone is a pollutant. Glossary.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-4

When

Launched on 1 July 2025; first images of air pollutants were released in October 2025.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-4 offers first glimpses of air pollutants

Where

A high orbit, about 22,000 miles up, where a satellite moves with Earth's spin and stays over the same spot. Glossary, watching Europe and northern Africa.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-4

Why

Air pollution changes through the day, and hourly data can improve air quality forecasts.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-4

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

Sentinel-4's first images showed pollution hotspots such as Italy's Po Valley. It joins NASA's TEMPO and Korea's GEMS in watching air quality hourly from geostationary The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-4 offers first glimpses of air pollutants

For Further Information: ESA, Sentinel-4

Sentinel-4 watches Europe's air pollution every hour from 36,000 km up.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Better hourly air quality forecasts help people with asthma and other conditions plan their day.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-4

For Nature

Tracking pollutants like nitrogen dioxide and ozone shows where emissions harm A community of living things and the place they live, like a reef or a forest, all depending on each other. Glossary and crops.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-4
