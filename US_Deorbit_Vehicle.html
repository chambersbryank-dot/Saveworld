U.S. Deorbit Vehicle (NASA / SpaceX)

NASA selected SpaceX to build the U.S. Deorbit Vehicle that will guide the International Space Station safely out of orbit after 2030.

The Five W's of U.S. Deorbit Vehicle

Who

NASA, with SpaceX developing the vehicle; the station's safe deorbit is the responsibility of all five partner space agencies.

Source: NASA, U.S. Deorbit Vehicle selection

What

A dedicated spacecraft that will provide the capability to deorbit the station in a controlled manner.

Source: NASA, U.S. Deorbit Vehicle selection

When

Station operations are planned through 2030; the deorbit follows the end of its operational life.

Source: NASA, U.S. Deorbit Vehicle selection

Source: NASA, U.S. Deorbit Vehicle selection

Why

A controlled end keeps the largest human-made object in orbit from becoming the largest piece of uncontrolled debris.

Source: NASA, U.S. Deorbit Vehicle selection

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active (contracted)

The International Space Station is the largest object humans have put in orbit, and it cannot simply be left to fall. NASA selected SpaceX to develop the U.S. Deorbit Vehicle, which will steer the station down in a controlled way after its operational life ends in 2030, avoiding risk to populated areas. NASA will own and operate the vehicle once it is built.

Source: NASA, U.S. Deorbit Vehicle selection
