How every lesson is built

  • Clear learning objectives
  • Prerequisites stated up front
  • Step-by-step derivations, with the math method named (calculus, vectors, ODEs, numerical methods)
  • Worked examples, then practice problems with solutions
  • Common mistakes to avoid
  • Cited textbooks and papers; every number computed and checked
