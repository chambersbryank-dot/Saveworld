Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS)

Europe's air monitoring service forecasts air pollution, smoke and dust worldwide twice a day; more than 23,500 users (CAMS, 2024).

Image: NASA Earth Observatory (Aqua MODIS, June 19, 2013).

  1. Home

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 5 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • CAMS checks the air all over the world, from smoke to dust to pollution.
  • It uses satellites and computer models to forecast dirty air days ahead.
  • People can plan to stay safe on bad air days.

The Five W's of CAMS

Who

The European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) runs CAMS for the European Commission, as one of six Copernicus services.

Source: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service, About us

What

Quality-assured information about the atmosphere, especially trace gases and Tiny bits of dust, smoke, salt or pollution floating in the air. They can block sunlight and help clouds form. Glossary, from satellites, ground stations and models.

Source: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service, About us

When

Global forecasts run twice daily, out to five days; European forecasts run daily, out to four days.

Source: CAMS, Introduction at the 8th General Assembly (June 12, 2024)

Where

The whole globe at 40 km, and Europe at 10 km.

Source: CAMS, Introduction at the 8th General Assembly (June 12, 2024)

Why

Its forecasts support air quality policies, and an aerosol-alerts service monitors natural air pollution events.

Source: CAMS, Introduction at the 8th General Assembly (June 12, 2024)

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

CAMS reported more than 23,500 users in June 2024, over 3,050 of them routine users (CAMS).

Source: CAMS, Introduction at the 8th General Assembly (June 12, 2024)

For Further Information: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service, About us

CAMS draws on more than 80 satellite instruments plus ground measurements (CAMS).

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

CAMS forecasts support air quality policies in Europe and analyse air pollution episodes (CAMS).

Source: CAMS, Introduction at the 8th General Assembly (June 12, 2024)

For Nature

CAMS tracks A gas, like carbon dioxide or methane, that traps heat in the air around Earth, the way a greenhouse keeps plants warm. Glossary, aerosols and smoke, which shape climate and A community of living things and the place they live, like a reef or a forest, all depending on each other. Glossary (CAMS).

Source: Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service, About us
