SPHEREx (NASA)

Launched 11 March 2025, NASA's SPHEREx maps the whole sky in 102 infrared colors and finished its first all-sky map in 2025 (NASA JPL).

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • SPHEREx is a space An instrument that collects light (or other energy) to see faraway things more clearly. Glossary that takes pictures of the whole sky.
  • It sees 102 colors of light that our eyes cannot see.
  • Those colors show where frozen water hides in space.

The Five W's of SPHEREx (NASA)

Who

NASA, with the mission managed by JPL and science led by Caltech.

Source: NASA Science, SPHEREx

What

A space telescope that maps the entire sky in 102 A kind of light our eyes can't see that we feel as heat. Special cameras use it to see warm objects and gases. Glossary wavelengths.

Source: NASA JPL, SPHEREx completes first cosmic map

When

Launched 11 March 2025; its first all-sky map was completed in December 2025.

Source: NASA JPL, SPHEREx completes first cosmic map

Where

Earth The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary, looking out at the whole sky.

Source: NASA Science, SPHEREx

Why

Its colors reveal water ice and other frozen ingredients for life in clouds where stars form.

Source: NASA JPL, SPHEREx completes first cosmic map

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

SPHEREx's all-sky map shows stars, hot hydrogen gas and cosmic dust, and other wavelengths trace water, A gas that people and animals breathe out and that burning fuel releases. Plants take it in, and too much of it in the air warms the planet. Glossary and carbon monoxide ices across our galaxy.

Source: NASA JPL, SPHEREx completes first cosmic map

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Its free maps are a resource for astronomers everywhere.

Source: NASA Science, SPHEREx

For Nature

Mapping where water ice exists in space helps explain where the water on planets like Earth came from.

Source: NASA JPL, SPHEREx completes first cosmic map
