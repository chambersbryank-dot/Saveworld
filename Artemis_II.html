Artemis II

Four astronauts, one loop around the Moon, and a new record for how far humans have traveled.

Image: NASA, Artemis II launch.

The Five W's of Artemis II

Who

NASA and the Canadian Space Agency, with a crew of four.

Source: NASA, Artemis II crew back on Earth

What

A crewed test flight of the Orion spacecraft and Space Launch System around the Moon.

Source: NASA, Artemis II mission

When

April 2026; the crew splashed down off San Diego after a nearly 10-day journey.

Source: NASA, Artemis II crew back on Earth

Where

A loop around the Moon and back, about 695,081 miles in all.

Source: NASA, Artemis II distance record

Why

To prove Orion with people aboard before Artemis III, NASA's next lunar mission.

Source: NASA, Artemis II mission

Impact Statement

Agency mission · complete; Artemis III next

Artemis II was the first crewed flight around the Moon in more than half a century. NASA astronauts Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover and Christina Koch and CSA astronaut Jeremy Hansen reached 252,756 miles from Earth on April 6, 2026, breaking Apollo 13's 1970 record, and flew 695,081 miles in total. It is the relationship between Earth and space at its most human: the farther we go, the more clearly we see home.

Source: NASA, Artemis II distance record

Source: NASA, Artemis II crew back on Earth

The Artemis II crew reached 252,756 miles from Earth on April 6, 2026, the farthest humans have ever traveled.
