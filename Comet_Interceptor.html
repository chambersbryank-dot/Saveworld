Comet Interceptor (ESA / JAXA)

Three spacecraft will wait in space for a fresh comet and fly past it together to make a 3D map; launch is planned for late 2028 or early 2029 (ESA).

Image: NASA/Bill Ingalls (Comet NEOWISE).

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 1 source cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Some comets come from very far away and have never been near the Sun before.
  • Comet Interceptor will wait in space until scientists find one.
  • Then three spacecraft fly past it together to make a 3D picture.

The Five W's of Comet Interceptor (ESA / JAXA)

Who

ESA leads the mission in collaboration with JAXA, which provides one of the probes.

Source: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet

What

A main spacecraft and two probes that observe a comet from several directions at the same time.

Source: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet

When

Launch planned for late 2028 or early 2029; the mission should finish within six years of launch.

Source: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet

Where

It parks at the Sun-Earth L2 point, 1.5 million km from Earth, then intercepts its target.

Source: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet

Why

A comet entering the inner Solar System for the first time still holds material from the dawn of the Solar System.

Source: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet

Impact Statement

Agency solution · planned

Comet Interceptor will be the first mission to visit a long-period comet as it journeys into the inner Solar System, or possibly an interstellar object.

Source: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet

For Further Information: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet

Comet Interceptor launches before its comet is even discovered, then waits in space to catch it.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Being ready to launch toward a newly found object is practice for responding quickly to surprises from space.

Source: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet

For Nature

Comets may have helped deliver water and other ingredients to the young Earth; a pristine comet shows them unchanged.

Source: ESA, Comet Interceptor factsheet
