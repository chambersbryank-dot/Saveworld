We develop and share solutions to save the planet minus the doom.

Saving Planets is a hopeful catalog of work already underway. The aim is simple: show a fix, show the number, and invite the next idea. No guilt. No apocalypse slide. Just things that move plastic, trees, and energy in the right direction.

What a solution looks like

Solar-powered data centers in orbit — unlimited energy, zero water cooling. Saturn, photographed by Cassini, is the picture we use for that idea.
