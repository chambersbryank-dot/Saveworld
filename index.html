Forests (17)

American Forests

Forest restoration, urban forestry and Tree Equity, which aims to bring trees to every neighborhood.

Eden: People+Planet

Eden: People+Planet restores landscapes with local communities, including a 394,000-hectare project in Tanzania's Rubeho Mountains (Eden).

GEDI Forest Lidar (NASA)

GEDI fires lasers from the International Space Station to measure the height and 3D shape of Earth's forests (University of Maryland).

Space

Satellites, missions and keeping orbit clean

Space (5)Project Rawane (12)Earth Observation (31)Planetary Defense (8)Space Weather (10)Space Disposal (14)

Astroscale

Astroscale inspects, services and removes debris in orbit: ELSA-d, ADRAS-J's 2024 close-up of an H-IIA upper stage, and ADRAS-J2, targeted for fiscal 2027.

Artemis II (NASA / CSA)

Artemis II carried four astronauts around the Moon in April 2026, reaching 252,756 miles from Earth, farther than any humans before.

ALOS-4 "DAICHI-4" (JAXA)

JAXA's DAICHI-4 radar sees through clouds over a 200 km swath, continuing the radar record used for deforestation alerts.

Solution of the Week

The Great Bubble Barrier

A curtain of bubbles across a canal lifts plastic to the surface and guides it into a catch system, while boats and fish pass freely. Their FAQ cites an 86 percent catch rate for floating plastic (company figure).
