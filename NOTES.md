# Notes

## OER Commons

Only a complete individual course goes to OER Commons. Never list the undergraduate studies hub there.

## Course depth bar

AERO 101 is the template standard for a course. A course at this depth includes explanations, instructor notes, a teacher guide, practice, quizzes, a midterm and a final, a self-assessment rubric, labs, a study guide, and openly licensed videos.

## Study video player

Every video on a study or course page uses the Plyr player: speed, cite (APA and MLA), a credit line with the license, and youtube-nocookie. Captions, an interactive transcript, text and PDF downloads, and chapter marks are included only when they come from a real caption source. If a timed transcript cannot be retrieved, the player says so and those controls are omitted. Do not invent captions, chapter times, or a publication date.

Muted autoplay, about five seconds after load, is only for the one featured film on a page. Every other film stays a poster until it is chosen. Posters and the player script load lazily. A page must not autoplay a stack of films.
