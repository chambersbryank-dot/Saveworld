The Ocean Cleanup

The largest cleanup in history — removing plastic from our oceans and rivers.

The Five W's of The Ocean Cleanup

Who

The Ocean Cleanup, a nonprofit founded by Boyan Slat, with engineers and scientists across more than a dozen countries.

What

Technologies that remove legacy plastic from garbage patches and intercept plastic in rivers before it reaches the sea.

When

Active for over ten years. Ten million kilograms removed by April 2024, fifteen million by July 2024.

Where

The Great Pacific Garbage Patch and about one thousand rivers that contribute most riverine plastic.

Why

Plastic harms marine wildlife, breaks into microplastics, and persists for decades. Cleanup protects the water we depend on.

Impact Statement

The stated mission is to clean up ninety percent of floating ocean plastic by 2040.
