Copernicus CO2M Carbon Dioxide Monitoring (ESA / EU)

A planned trio of satellites that will measure the carbon dioxide and methane people release, so countries can check whether their emission cuts are working (ESA).

The Five W's of Copernicus CO2M Carbon Dioxide Monitoring (ESA / EU)

Who

ESA is developing CO2M for the European Union's Copernicus program. OHB System AG builds the satellites, with Thales Alenia Space providing the instruments.

Source: ESA, Europe’s carbon dioxide monitoring mission elevated to the power of three

What

Each satellite carries a near-infrared and shortwave-infrared spectrometer that measures carbon dioxide and A greenhouse gas that traps much more heat than carbon dioxide over a few decades. It leaks from oil and gas sites, landfills and farms. Glossary in the air, focusing on emissions from human activity. It also measures nitrogen dioxide and tiny airborne particles.

Source: ESA, CO2M mission overview

When

The first satellite, CO2M-A, is planned for launch in 2027. CO2M-B is expected about six months later, and CO2M-C about a year after that.

Source: Copernicus SentiWiki, CO2M

Where

In a sun-synchronous The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary about 735 km above Earth. With three satellites, CO2M will measure around the whole globe in about 3.5 days.

Source: Copernicus SentiWiki, CO2M

Why

Countries need an independent way to see whether their climate policies are cutting emissions and meeting Paris Agreement targets. CO2M will estimate emissions at local, national and regional scales.

Source: ESA, Copernicus Sentinel Expansion missions

Impact Statement

Agency solution · planned

ESA and the European Commission expanded CO2M from two satellites to three, cutting the time to measure carbon dioxide around the whole globe to about 3.5 days.

Source: ESA, Europe’s carbon dioxide monitoring mission elevated to the power of three

For Further Information: ESA, Copernicus Sentinel Expansion missions

Three CO2M satellites will measure human-made carbon dioxide around the whole globe in about 3.5 days.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Independent measurements of emissions from cities, power plants and countries help governments see which climate policies work, so effort goes where it protects people most.

Source: ESA, Copernicus Sentinel Expansion missions

For Nature

Cutting carbon dioxide and methane slows climate change, which threatens forests, oceans and wildlife. CO2M will show where those cuts are really happening.

Source: ESA, Copernicus Sentinel Expansion missions
