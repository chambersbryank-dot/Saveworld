Copernicus Sentinel-2C (ESA)

Sentinel-2C joined the Copernicus fleet in September 2024, imaging land and coasts in 13 bands so forests and crops are checked every five days.

Image: NASA.

Copernicus Sentinel-2C: Mission Status

Active. Dates per ESA; missions shift.

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • What is it? A satellite with a multispectral imager covering 13 spectral bands, with a 290 km swath and detail down to 10 m.
  • Why does it matter? To monitor agriculture, forests, land-use change, coastal and inland waters, and disasters.
  • How does it help people? Farmers use Sentinel-2 data to monitor crop health, predict yields and practice precision farming, and responders use it to map floods, wildfires and eruptions.

The Five W's of Copernicus Sentinel-2C

Who

ESA, for the EU’s Copernicus programme, funded by ESA Member States and the European Union.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-2 facts and figures

What

A satellite with a multispectral imager covering 13 spectral bands, with a 290 km swath and detail down to 10 m.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-2 facts and figures

When

Launched September 5, 2024 on the final Vega rocket from French Guiana.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-2C joins the Copernicus family in orbit

Where

A polar, Sun-synchronous The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary 786 km up, covering land and coasts between 84°N and 84°S.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-2 facts and figures

Why

To monitor agriculture, forests, land-use change, coastal and inland waters, and disasters.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-2 facts and figures

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

Sentinel-2 revisits the same spot every five days at the equator with its two-satellite constellation. Its data support crop health monitoring and yield prediction, water quality monitoring, and disaster management for wildfires, volcanic eruptions and floods.

Source: ESA, Sentinel-2 facts and figures

Source: ESA, Sentinel-2C joins the Copernicus family in orbit
