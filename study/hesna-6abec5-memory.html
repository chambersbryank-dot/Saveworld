Original practice scenes, not real HPD exam items. Levels 1–4 are the streets you already have. Level 5 is new: four storefronts, eight people, five vehicles, 90 seconds. Level 6 is new: an incident with a correction, picture and paragraph together. Study the scene, then it hides. Score 80% to unlock the next level. Score 95% or higher and that level is done: what remains on it is optional review, and the next level is the one that counts.
