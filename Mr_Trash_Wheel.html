Mr. Trash Wheel Family

Baltimore's Trash Wheels intercept about 500 tons of trash a year before it reaches the harbor and the Chesapeake Bay (organization figure).

Image: NASA.

The Five W's of Mr. Trash Wheel Family

Who

The Waterfront Partnership of Baltimore and its Healthy Harbor Initiative.

Source: Mr. Trash Wheel

What

Four trash interceptors that use the current and solar power to lift litter out of the water.

Source: Mr. Trash Wheel

When

Ongoing; about 500 tons collected each year (organization figure).

Source: Mr. Trash Wheel

Where

Stream and river mouths around Baltimore's Inner Harbor, Maryland.

Source: Mr. Trash Wheel, trash interception (organization figure)

Why

Stopping trash at the river mouth keeps it out of the harbor, the Chesapeake Bay and the ocean.

Source: Mr. Trash Wheel, trash interception (organization figure)

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

Rain washes litter from city streets into streams and out to sea. Baltimore's Trash Wheels catch it first. The Waterfront Partnership of Baltimore's four wheels collect about 500 tons of trash and debris from the harbor each year (organization figure).

Source: Mr. Trash Wheel, trash interception (organization figure)
