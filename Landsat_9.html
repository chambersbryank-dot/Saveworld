Landsat 9 (NASA / USGS)

Landsat 9 continues a 50-year record of Earth’s land, collecting 740 scenes a day and, with Landsat 8, seeing each place every 8 days.

Image: NASA (KSC-20210927-PH-KLS02_0069).

Landsat 9: Mission Status

Active. Dates per NASA; missions shift.

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • What is it? The latest Landsat satellite, collecting 740 scenes of Earth each day, almost double its minimum requirement.
  • Why does it matter? The longest continuous satellite record of Earth’s land surface shows how forests, water and farms change.
  • How does it help people? Landsat supports water management in drought-prone regions where farms, industry and communities compete for water, and its free data added an estimated $25.6 billion to the U.S. economy in 2023.

The Five W's of Landsat 9

Who

NASA and the U.S. Geological Survey.

Source: NASA, Landsat 9

What

The latest Landsat satellite, collecting 740 scenes of Earth each day, almost double its minimum requirement.

Source: NASA, Landsat 9

When

Launched in 2021, extending a record that began more than 50 years ago.

Source: NASA, Landsat 9

Where

Orbiting with Landsat 8, so the pair observes the same location on Earth every 8 days.

Source: NASA, Landsat 9

Why

The longest continuous satellite record of Earth’s land surface shows how forests, water and farms change.

Source: NASA, Landsat 9

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

Near-weekly Landsat observations enable tropical deforestation alerts, water quality monitoring and crop condition reports. NASA says Landsat’s free, public data contributed an estimated $25.6 billion to the U.S. economy in 2023 alone.

Source: NASA, Landsat 9

For Further Information: NASA, Landsat 9

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Landsat supports water management in drought-prone regions where farms, industry and communities compete for water, and its free data added an estimated $25.6 billion to the U.S. economy in 2023.

Source: NASA, Landsat 9

For Nature

With Landsat 8, it delivers near-weekly tropical deforestation alerts and water quality monitoring, including in the Great Lakes. See also Forests and Lakes.

Source: NASA, Landsat 9

Want to go deeper? NASA's Landsat 9 page has images, science and mission news.
