Polar

NASA GSFC, Operation IceBridge (2015)NASA Earth Observatory/Lauren Dauphin, Landsat 8 data from USGS (Thwaites Glacier, Dec. 28, 2019)NASA astronaut Loral O'Hara (iss070e086805, Labrador coast, Feb. 3, 2024, public domain)U.S. Department of State (Adélie penguin at Cape Royds, Antarctica, 2016, public domain)NASA Earth Observatory/Joshua Stevens, Landsat 9 data from USGS (Nordaustlandet, Svalbard, July 29, 2022)

Impact Statement

A decade of CryoSat data revealed 85 unknown lakes under Antarctica, raising the known active subglacial lakes by more than half, to 231 (ESA).

Source: ESA, 85 new subglacial lakes detected below Antarctica

CIMR will measure 95% of the globe every day, with sub-daily coverage of the polar regions (ESA).

Source: ESA, CIMR facts and figures

Heat absorbed in the tropics flows to the poles and escapes to space there. PREFIRE measures that far-infrared heat (NASA JPL).

Source: NASA JPL, PREFIRE mission extended

The Five W's of Polar Solutions

Who

The European Space Agency (ESA), NASA and the Canadian Space Agency, with their partners.

What

Satellites that measure polar ice from space: sea-ice thickness, the height of ice sheets and glaciers, lakes hidden under the ice, and the heat the poles send to space.

When

Now and next: ICESat-2 has measured ice since its launch on September 15, 2018 (NASA), and CRISTAL will continue CryoSat's ice record (ESA).

Source: NASA, ICESat-2

Source: ESA, CRISTAL prepares to sparkle

Where

The polar regions, including Antarctica, the Arctic Ocean, Greenland and Iceland, seen from satellites in polar orbits.

Why

Lakes under the ice affect how fast glaciers move and so influence global sea level rise (ESA). Daily sea-ice data also support safer work and travel in polar seas (ESA).

Source: ESA, 85 new subglacial lakes detected below Antarctica

Source: ESA, CIMR facts and figures

Missions and Solutions

North Pole: The Arctic · South Pole: Antarctica · Both Poles

North Pole: The Arctic

Sea ice, Greenland, Iceland, Svalbard and the Arctic Ocean.

Active🧊 Polar

Svalbard Global Seed Vault

The Svalbard Global Seed Vault stores backup copies of the world's crop seeds free of charge, more than 100 metres inside an Arctic mountain (Seed Vault).

Active🧊 Polar

Carbfix: Turning CO2 Into Stone

Carbfix dissolves carbon dioxide in water and injects it into basalt rock, where it turns into stone (company).

Active🧊 Polar

Iceland Land Restoration (Land and Forest Iceland)

Since 1907, Iceland's soil conservation agency, now Land and Forest Iceland, has worked with farmers to halt erosion and restore land (Government of Iceland).

Active🧊 Polar

PROMICE and GC-Net: Greenland Ice-Sheet Weather Stations

PROMICE and GC-Net put automatic weather stations on the Greenland ice sheet and share the data openly (PROMICE).

Active🧊 Polar

Arctic Weather Satellite and EPS-Sterna (ESA/EUMETSAT)

ESA's small Arctic Weather Satellite, launched in August 2024, paved the way for EPS-Sterna, a fleet of 20 satellites from 2029 (ESA, EUMETSAT).

Active🧊 Polar

Polar Bears International

Polar Bears International works through research, policy and on-the-ground conservation to protect polar bears and their sea-ice habitat (Polar Bears International).

Active🧊 Polar

SmartICE: Sea-Ice Safety for Northern Communities

SmartICE combines Indigenous and local knowledge of ice with sensors and satellite mapping to give near real-time ice-thickness information for safe travel (SmartICE).

Active🧊 Polar

Permafrost Pathways

Permafrost Pathways connects science, people and policy to address Arctic permafrost thaw and support affected communities (Permafrost Pathways).

Active🧊 Polar

Tallurutiup Imanga National Marine Conservation Area

Tallurutiup Imanga, in Nunavut, protects Arctic waters where narwhals live alongside thousands of seabirds, next to Inuit communities (Parks Canada).

Active🧊 Polar

PAME: Protecting the Arctic Marine Environment

PAME is the Arctic Council working group for the protection and sustainable use of the Arctic marine environment (PAME).

Active🧊 Polar

CAFF: Conservation of Arctic Flora and Fauna

CAFF is the biodiversity working group of the Arctic Council (CAFF).

Active🧊 Polar

MOSAiC Expedition: A Year Frozen in the Arctic Ice

MOSAiC was the first year-round expedition into the central Arctic to study its climate system (MOSAiC).

Active🧊 Polar

WWF Arctic Programme

Since 1992, WWF's Global Arctic Programme has worked for a well-managed, biodiverse and resilient Arctic (WWF Arctic).

Active🧊 Polar

Inuit Circumpolar Council

Founded in 1977, the Inuit Circumpolar Council brings Inuit rights and priorities to the international level (ICC).

Active🧊 Polar

Arctic Basecamp

Arctic Basecamp is a registered not-for-profit that spotlights the global threats of polar climate change and communicates areas of action (Arctic Basecamp).

South Pole: Antarctica

Antarctica, its ice and the Southern Ocean.

Active🧊 Polar

International Thwaites Glacier Collaboration

About 100 scientists from the UK, US, South Korea, Germany and Sweden are working out how fast Antarctica's Thwaites Glacier could raise sea levels (ITGC).

Active🧊 Polar

Ross Sea Region Marine Protected Area

New Zealand and the United States won unanimous agreement to protect the Ross Sea, and the MPA entered into force on 1 December 2017 (NZ MFAT).

Active🧊 Polar

South Georgia Habitat Restoration

Two charities raised £10 million to clear rats and mice from South Georgia, and on 8 May 2018 the island was declared rodent-free (SGHT).

Active🧊 Polar

Gough Island Restoration Programme

The RSPB-led Gough Island Restoration Programme tried in 2021 to remove the invasive mice that prey on seabirds; mice were found again, so the eradication did not fully succeed (RSPB).

Active🧊 Polar

Penguin Watch: Count Penguins from Home

On Penguin Watch, anyone online counts penguin adults, chicks and eggs in camera photos to help scientists understand their lives and environment (Zooniverse).

Active🧊 Polar

Oceanites: Antarctic Penguin Census

For more than 30 years, Oceanites has tracked penguin populations on the rapidly warming Antarctic Peninsula and shares the data openly (Oceanites).

Active🧊 Polar

SOCCOM: Robot Floats in the Southern Ocean

SOCCOM is a multi-institutional program focused on the Southern Ocean and its influence on climate, using floats and models (SOCCOM).

Active🧊 Polar

SOOS: Southern Ocean Observing System

SOOS facilitates and enhances global Southern Ocean observations by coordinating fieldwork and sharing data (SOOS).

Active🧊 Polar

Beyond EPICA – Oldest Ice

The European project Beyond EPICA – Oldest Ice drills Antarctic ice to read climate and environmental history (Beyond EPICA).

Active🧊 Polar

ASOC: Antarctic and Southern Ocean Coalition

ASOC, a coalition of conservation groups, has advocated for stronger protection of Antarctica and the Southern Ocean since 1978 (ASOC).

Active🧊 Polar

CCAMLR: Conserving Antarctic Marine Life

CCAMLR manages Antarctic marine life with an ecosystem-based approach, allowing only sustainable harvesting that accounts for effects on the ecosystem (CCAMLR).

Active🧊 Polar

SCAR: Scientific Committee on Antarctic Research

SCAR initiates, develops and coordinates international scientific research in the Antarctic region, including the Southern Ocean (SCAR).

Active🧊 Polar

IAATO: Responsible Antarctic Tourism

IAATO, founded in 1991, promotes safe and environmentally responsible private-sector travel to the Antarctic (IAATO).

Both Poles

Satellites that watch the ice at both ends of the Earth.

Image: ESA–S. Wandzilak

Active🧊 Polar

CryoSat Ice Mission (ESA)

CryoSat measures polar sea ice and the great ice sheets from space, and its data revealed 85 hidden lakes under Antarctica (ESA).

Image: NASA/George Hale

Active🧊 Polar

CRISTAL Polar Ice and Snow Altimeter (ESA)

CRISTAL will carry the first dual-frequency radar altimeter to measure polar sea-ice thickness and the snow on top, following ESA's CryoSat (ESA).

Active🧊 Polar

CIMR Copernicus Imaging Microwave Radiometer (ESA)

Two Copernicus satellites will measure sea ice, sea-surface temperature and salinity over 95% of the globe every day, with sub-daily polar views (ESA).

Active🧊 Polar

ICESat-2 (NASA)

NASA's ICESat-2 laser fires 10,000 pulses a second to measure ice sheets, sea ice, forests and coastal seafloor in 3D.

Active🧊 Polar

PREFIRE Polar CubeSats (NASA)

Two tiny CubeSats measuring how much heat the Arctic and Antarctica release to space; extended through at least September 2026 (NASA JPL).

Active🌍 Earth Observation

RADARSAT Constellation Mission (Canada)

Three radar satellites that see through cloud, watching sea ice, ships and floods; free public images (Canadian Space Agency).

Active🧊 Polar

GCOM-W "SHIZUKU" Sea-Ice Watch (JAXA)

JAXA's SHIZUKU satellite, launched in 2012, measures sea ice, rain, snow and sea temperature through clouds, day and night (JAXA).

Active🧊 Polar

SCISAT Ozone Watch (Canadian Space Agency)

Canada's 150 kg SCISAT has measured the chemistry of the ozone layer since 2003, with a special focus on Canada and the Arctic (CSA).
