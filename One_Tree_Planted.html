One Tree Planted

Since 2014, One Tree Planted has planted more than 171 million trees with 489 partners in 84 countries (organization figure).

Image: NASA/JPL-Caltech.

  1. Home
  2. Forests
  3. One Tree Planted

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 2 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • One Tree Planted is a group that plants trees all over the world.
  • They work with local helpers and check on the trees for three years to make sure they grow.
  • They have planted more than 171 million trees in 84 countries.

The Five W's of One Tree Planted

Who

One Tree Planted, a nonprofit based in Shelburne, Vermont, working with local planting partners.

Source: One Tree Planted, About Us

What

Planting trees where a forest was lost so it can grow back. Glossary and agroforestry projects, each followed by three years of monitoring to check tree survival.

Source: One Tree Planted, About Us

When

Since 2014.

Source: One Tree Planted, About Us

Where

84 countries across North America, Latin America, Africa, Asia, Europe and Oceania.

Source: One Tree Planted, About Us

Why

Trees give clean air, water and soil, store carbon and hold land together against erosion.

Source: One Tree Planted, About Us

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

One Tree Planted reports more than 171 million trees planted with 489 partners across 84 countries since 2014 (organization figures).

Source: One Tree Planted, About Us

For Further Information: One Tree Planted, About Us

One Tree Planted and its partners have planted more than 171 million trees in 84 countries.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Agroforestry projects grow food and income for communities, improving food security and livelihoods.

Source: One Tree Planted, About Us

For Nature

Projects restore forest habitat for thousands of threatened species and help after forest fires.

Source: One Tree Planted, About Us
