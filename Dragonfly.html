Dragonfly Titan Rotorcraft (NASA)

NASA's Dragonfly is a car-size rotorcraft that will fly across Saturn's moon Titan to study the chemistry that came before life (NASA).

Image: NASA.

  1. Home

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 5 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Dragonfly is like a giant drone with eight rotors.
  • It will fly to Titan, a moon of Saturn with a thick, hazy sky.
  • It will look for the kinds of chemistry that came before life on Earth.

The Five W's of Dragonfly

Who

NASA, with the rotorcraft designed and built at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory. It is a mission of NASA's New Frontiers program.

Source: NASA Science, Dragonfly

What

The first rotorcraft to explore another world. It has eight rotors and flies like a large drone, stopping to sample the surface.

Source: NASA, Dragonfly mission confirmed (Apr 2024)

When

Launch is targeted for July 5–25, 2028, on a SpaceX Falcon Heavy, with arrival at Titan in 2034.

Source: NASA, Dragonfly launch contract

Where

Saturn's moon Titan, including its dunes and Selk Crater. It will launch from NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

Source: NASA Science, Dragonfly

Why

Titan's chemistry may resemble early Earth before life began. Dragonfly will study that prebiotic chemistry.

Source: NASA, Dragonfly mission confirmed (Apr 2024)

Impact Statement

Agency solution · planned

Dragonfly is planned to explore Titan for 3.3 years, flying to many sites instead of staying in one place (NASA).

Source: NASA Science, Dragonfly

For Further Information: NASA Science, Dragonfly

Dragonfly plans one flight every one or two Titan days, and one Titan day lasts about 16 Earth days.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Studying chemistry that came before biology helps us understand how life could have started on Earth (NASA).

Source: NASA Science, Dragonfly

For Nature

Learning how planets and moons build life's ingredients helps us understand and value Earth's own living systems.

Source: NASA Science, Dragonfly
