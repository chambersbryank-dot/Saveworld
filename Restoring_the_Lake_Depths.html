The Five W's of Restoring the Lake Depths

Who

A certified Nevada 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2020 by Lindsay Kopf and her parents Jamie and Kathy Kopf, based in Zephyr Cove, Nevada. The only organization using remotely operated vehicles with a gripper tool for deep-water litter cleanup and surveying.

What

A fleet of four ROVs — including the flagship Deep Emerald with a 600-meter fiber optic tether — plus volunteer scuba and free divers, removing submerged debris from Lake Tahoe's depths. They also provide high-resolution underwater surveying photography and videography.

When

Founded in 2020. In the summer of 2024 alone, they removed over 13 tons of trash and debris from Lake Tahoe's south shore. In a single year they have recovered roughly 80 tires and nearly a ton of plastic.

Source: Restoring the Lake Depths

Where

Lake Tahoe, Nevada and California — the only lake they serve, but at depths no diver can reach: Deep Emerald can explore down to 1,640 feet.

Why

Lake Tahoe looks pristine from the surface, but abandoned fishing gear, plastic, and debris accumulate below, harming the ecosystem and the lake's legendary clarity. Removing it restores the lake to its original splendor.
