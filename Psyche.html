Psyche (NASA)

NASA's Psyche used a Mars flyby on 15 May 2026 to head for a metal-rich asteroid it will orbit from August 2029 (NASA).

Image: NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU (PIA24473).

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Psyche is a NASA spacecraft flying to an A rocky object that orbits the Sun, much smaller than a planet. Most are in a belt between Mars and Jupiter. Glossary that might be made of a lot of metal.
  • In 2026 it swung past Mars to get a speed boost.
  • It gets there in 2029 and will help us learn what the center of a planet is like.

The Five W's of Psyche (NASA)

Who

NASA, with the mission led by Arizona State University and managed by NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory.

Source: NASA, Psyche aces Mars flyby

What

An orbiter that will map asteroid Psyche, which may be the partial metal core of an early planet building block.

Source: NASA Science, Psyche mission overview

When

Launched 13 October 2023; Mars flyby 15 May 2026; arrival August 2029.

Source: NASA Science, Psyche mission overview

Where

The outer main asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter.

Source: NASA Science, Psyche mission overview

Why

We cannot visit Earth's own metal core, so a metal-rich asteroid is a rare window into how planets form.

Source: NASA Science, Psyche mission overview

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

The Mars flyby gave Psyche a boost of about 1,000 miles per hour without using propellant. The spacecraft will spend about two years orbiting the 173-mile-wide asteroid.

Source: NASA, Psyche aces Mars flyby

For Further Information: NASA, Psyche aces Mars flyby

NASA's Psyche is flying to an asteroid that may be the leftover metal core of a baby planet.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Psyche uses solar-electric propulsion, efficient thrusters that help future missions travel farther on less fuel.

Source: NASA, Psyche aces Mars flyby

For Nature

Learning how metal cores form helps explain Earth's own core, which powers the magnetic field that protects life.

Source: NASA Science, Psyche mission overview
