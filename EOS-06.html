EOS-06 / Oceansat-3 (ISRO)

India's EOS-06, the third Oceansat, tracks ocean color, sea-surface temperature and winds; it imaged Cyclone Mandous and an algal bloom on day one.

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

ISRO's EOS-06 is the third-generation satellite in the Oceansat series, launched on PSLV-C54 in November 2022. Its Ocean Color Monitor, Sea Surface Temperature Monitor and Ku-band scatterometer keep ocean color and wind data flowing for operational use; early images captured Cyclone Mandous and an algal When tiny water plants called algae grow out of control. Some make toxins that can harm people, pets and fish. Glossary.
