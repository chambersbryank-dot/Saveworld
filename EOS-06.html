The Five W's of EOS-06

Who

Source: ISRO, EOS-06

What

An ocean satellite with color, temperature and wind sensors.

Source: ISRO, EOS-06

When

Launched on PSLV-C54 in November 2022; operating.

Source: ISRO, EOS-06

Where

Earth orbit, watching the world's oceans.

Source: ISRO, EOS-06

Why

Continuity of ocean color and wind data for fisheries, cyclones and coasts.

Source: ISRO, EOS-06

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

ISRO's EOS-06 is the third-generation satellite in the Oceansat series, launched on PSLV-C54 in November 2022. Its Ocean Color Monitor, Sea Surface Temperature Monitor and Ku-band scatterometer keep ocean color and wind data flowing for operational use; early images captured Cyclone Mandous and an algal bloom.

Source: ISRO, EOS-06
