BepiColombo (ESA / JAXA)

After eight years in space, BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury on 21 November 2026, the first mission to put two spacecraft there (ESA).

Image: NASA/JHUAPL/Carnegie Institution (MESSENGER).

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Mercury is the planet closest to the Sun.
  • BepiColombo is really two spacecraft that rode there together for eight years.
  • In 2026 they start circling Mercury to learn what it is made of.

The Five W's of BepiColombo (ESA / JAXA)

Who

The European Space Agency (ESA) and the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA).

Source: ESA, Latest updates: BepiColombo's arrival at Mercury

What

Two science orbiters: ESA's Mercury Planetary Orbiter and JAXA's Mio, which studies Mercury's magnetic field.

Source: NASA Science, BepiColombo

When

Launched in October 2018; The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary insertion is planned for 21 November 2026 and science for April 2027.

Source: ESA, Latest updates: BepiColombo's arrival at Mercury

Where

Mercury, the planet closest to the Sun.

Source: ESA, Latest updates: BepiColombo's arrival at Mercury

Why

Mercury's large iron core and magnetic field hold clues to how rocky planets like Earth formed.

Source: NASA Science, BepiColombo

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

BepiColombo will be the first mission to orbit Mercury with two spacecraft at once, measuring the surface, interior and magnetic field from different vantage points.

Source: ESA, Latest updates: BepiColombo's arrival at Mercury

For Further Information: ESA, Latest updates: BepiColombo's arrival at Mercury

In late 2026, BepiColombo becomes the first mission to put two spacecraft in orbit around Mercury.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Understanding how the Sun's wind hits a small planet's magnetic field helps scientists understand the space weather that reaches Earth.

Source: NASA Science, BepiColombo

For Nature

Comparing Mercury with Earth shows what makes our own planet's magnetic shield and climate so special.

Source: NASA Science, BepiColombo

Want to go deeper? ESA has live updates on the arrival.
