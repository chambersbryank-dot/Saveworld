4ocean

Full-time cleanup crews removing plastic from oceans, rivers, and coastlines — funded by products made from recovered plastic.

The Five W's of 4ocean

Who

4ocean, a for-profit company founded in 2017 by Alex Schulze and Andrew Cooper, with full-time cleanup crews operating in more than a dozen countries.

What

Daily cleanup operations pulling plastic from oceans, rivers, and coastlines, funded by products made from the recovered plastic — bracelets, sunglasses, and apparel sold through 4ocean.com.

When

Active since 2017. Surpassed 50 million pounds of plastic removed in February 2026, independently verified by GreenCircle. Over 29,000 cleanups completed and more than 2,400 jobs created.

Where

Crews work coastlines and waterways across the United States, the Caribbean, Central America, Southeast Asia, and Europe — wherever plastic is washing ashore or flowing downstream.

Why

Plastic in the ocean harms marine life, breaks into microplastics, and persists for centuries. Removing it at the source protects ecosystems and the communities that depend on clean water.

Impact Statement

Every product sold funds a cleanup — the model turns consumer purchases directly into pounds of plastic removed from the water. The 50-million-pound milestone in February 2026 was independently verified, and the company continues expanding crew operations worldwide.
