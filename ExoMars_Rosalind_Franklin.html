ExoMars Rosalind Franklin Rover: Mission Status

Launch targeted for 2028. Dates per ESA; missions shift.

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • What is it? Rosalind Franklin, the first European rover and the first mission able to both travel across Mars and study it at depth, with an onboard laboratory.
  • Why does it matter? To address whether life has ever existed on Mars.
  • How does it help people? ExoMars tackles a question that belongs to everyone: has life ever existed beyond Earth? The rover carries next-generation instruments in an onboard laboratory to look for the answer.

The Five W's of ExoMars Rosalind Franklin Rover

Who

ESA, through its ExoMars programme.

Source: ESA, ExoMars

What

Rosalind Franklin, the first European rover and the first mission able to both travel across Mars and study it at depth, with an onboard laboratory.

Source: ESA, ExoMars rover

When

The rover mission will target launch in 2028. The first ExoMars mission, the Trace Gas Orbiter, launched in 2016.

Source: ESA, ExoMars

Where

A Martian landing site chosen for its high potential to hold well-preserved organic material from the planet’s early history.

Source: ESA, ExoMars rover

Why

To address whether life has ever existed on Mars.

Source: ESA, ExoMars

Impact Statement

Agency solution · launch targeted for 2028

Rosalind Franklin will collect samples with a drill reaching 2 meters below the surface and analyze them in its onboard laboratory. Underground samples are more likely to keep biomarkers, because the thin Martian atmosphere offers little protection from radiation at the surface. The rover is expected to travel several kilometers.

Source: ESA, ExoMars rover

For Further Information: ESA, ExoMars

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

ExoMars tackles a question that belongs to everyone: has life ever existed beyond Earth? The rover carries next-generation instruments in an onboard laboratory to look for the answer.

Source: ESA, ExoMars

Source: ESA, ExoMars rover

For Nature

Mars shows what happens to a world that lost its protective atmosphere: its surface is exposed to radiation that can destroy organic material. Learning that story helps us value the atmosphere that shields life on Earth.

Source: ESA, ExoMars rover

Want to go deeper? ESA's ExoMars pages have rover tests, the landing site and mission news.
