DELPOE visual practice, not real exam items. Week 1 pictures and street sketches, questions included, are inside the study pack on each period. This page is Week 2: Quick Stop, then the corner, then the later sketches. It opens only after every Week 1 period is mastered, and it is harder. Study each picture for 2 minutes. No notes. Then it hides. Every question is answered by what that picture shows.
