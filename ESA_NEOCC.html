Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre (ESA)

Europe's asteroid watch centre computes orbits and impact risks every day and keeps a public list of every asteroid with a non-zero chance of hitting Earth: 2,044 at last count (ESA).

Image: NASA/JPL-Caltech (PIA26384).

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 4 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Some A rocky object that orbits the Sun, much smaller than a planet. Most are in a belt between Mars and Jupiter. Glossary pass near Earth, and scientists need to know where they will go.
  • ESA's centre in Italy collects An instrument that collects light (or other energy) to see faraway things more clearly. Glossary data and works out their paths.
  • If an asteroid ever looks dangerous, they warn emergency teams.

The Five W's of NEOCC

Who

The European Space Agency's An asteroid or comet whose orbit brings it close to Earth's path around the Sun. Scientists track them to make sure none is on course to hit us. Glossary Coordination Centre, the operational heart of its Planetary Defence Office.

Source: ESA, Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre

What

Every day it provides The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary information, impact monitoring, data and risk analysis for asteroids and comets.

Source: ESA, Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre

When

Every day, using information gathered from across the globe.

Source: ESA, Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre

Where

ESA's ESRIN site in Frascati, Italy, linked to a network of European data sources.

Source: ESA, Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre

Why

To evaluate and monitor the threat from objects that come near Earth, and propose mitigation if needed, for example to emergency agencies.

Source: ESA, Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

ESA's public risk list currently holds 2,044 near-Earth asteroids for which a non-zero impact probability has been computed (ESA NEOCC).

Source: ESA NEOCC, Risk list

For Further Information: ESA, Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre

Each risk list entry shows the asteroid's riskiest Earth approach, with its date, size, speed and probability (ESA NEOCC).

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

If an impact ever threatens, the centre can advise emergency response agencies so people can be protected (ESA).

Source: ESA, Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre

For Nature

Planetary defence protects Earth, its inhabitants and the infrastructure we depend on (ESA).

Source: ESA, Near-Earth Object Coordination Centre
