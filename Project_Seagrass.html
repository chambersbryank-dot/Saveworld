Project Seagrass

Project Seagrass, a UK charity, has planted more than 150,000 seagrass seeds to bring back underwater meadows (organization figures).

The Five W's of Project Seagrass

Who

Project Seagrass, a registered charity in England and Wales (1162824) and Scotland (SC046788).

Source: Project Seagrass

What

Protecting and restoring seagrass meadows through community work, research and planting.

Source: Project Seagrass

When

Working since 2013; it reports more than 150,000 seeds planted and 3,000+ new seagrass data points.

Source: Project Seagrass

Where

Based in Bridgend, Wales, United Kingdom, working from local shores to international policy.

Source: Project Seagrass

Why

Many seagrass meadows are changing by around 1–2% each year, and small losses add up to big declines.

Source: Project Seagrass

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

Project Seagrass reports more than 150,000 seagrass seeds planted and more than 3,000 new seagrass data points (organization figures).

Source: Project Seagrass

For Further Information: Project Seagrass

Seagrass is not seaweed: it is a flowering plant that forms underwater meadows.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Seagrass meadows support fisheries, stabilize coastlines and improve water quality for coastal communities.

Source: Project Seagrass

For Nature

The meadows shelter marine life and store carbon, so restoring them helps the sea and the climate.

Source: Project Seagrass
