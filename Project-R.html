Draft: ADRAS-J2

ADRAS-J2 is Astroscale’s follow-on debris removal mission, Phase II of JAXA’s CRD2 program, worth about 13.2 billion yen. It targets the same Japanese H-IIA rocket upper stage ADRAS-J photographed in 2024 — an eleven-meter, three-ton object never designed to be captured. ADRAS-J2 will use a robotic arm to capture that non-cooperative debris and lower its orbit so it burns up in the atmosphere. Launch is targeted for fiscal 2027, riding Isar Aerospace’s Spectrum rocket from Norway. It is the mission that turns Astroscale’s inspection demo into the world’s first removal of legacy debris.

Draft: Project Rawane

Project Rawane explores the two-way relationship between Earth and space — how what we do in space protects and improves life on the ground, and how what we do on the ground shapes what happens above us. Space-based solar power, orbital debris removal, and Earth observation satellites are space work that pays off on the planet. Plastic cleanup, sustainable energy, and conservation are planetary work that keeps space worth protecting. Named for Rawane — because the best projects start with someone you love.
