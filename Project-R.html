Project R

The two-way relationship between Earth and space.

The Five W's of Project R

Who

Saving Planets, highlighting Project Rawane as a space-and-planet initiative.

What

Project Rawane is the two-way relationship between Earth and space — space work like debris removal, space-based solar power, and Earth observation that protects life on the ground, and planetary work like cleanup and conservation that keeps orbits usable.

When

Ongoing, with the flagship mission ADRAS-J2 targeted for fiscal twenty twenty-seven.

Where

Low Earth orbit, Norway's launch site, and everywhere the debris and its effects reach.

Why

Space and Earth are one system. Debris threatens the satellites we rely on, and what we do on the ground shapes what happens above us. Protecting one protects the other.

Impact Statement

Project Rawane is named for Rawane — because the best projects start with someone you love. It follows Astroscale's ADRAS-J mission, which photographed an eleven-meter, three-ton Japanese H-two-A rocket upper stage in twenty twenty-four — an object never designed to be captured. ADRAS-J2, Phase Two of JAXA's CRD2 program worth about thirteen point two billion yen, will use a robotic arm to capture that same non-cooperative debris and lower its orbit so it burns up in the atmosphere. Launch is targeted for fiscal twenty twenty-seven on Isar Aerospace's Spectrum rocket from Norway. It is the mission that turns an inspection demo into the world's first removal of legacy debris — proof that the relationship between Earth and space runs both ways.
