The Five W's of Lake Plastic Recovery

Who

This is Saving Planets' own lake solution — a simple, verifiable rule for any lake cleanup project: recover plastic from freshwater lakes before it travels downstream.

What

Pulling plastic and litter out of lakes — surface skimming, shoreline collection, and diver-assisted recovery — so it never reaches rivers and oceans.

When

Ongoing — any lake cleanup project can adopt this approach today.

Where

Freshwater lakes worldwide: mountain lakes, urban lakes, and large systems like the Great Lakes.

Why

Lake plastic sinks out of sight, where it leaches fuel, oil, and heavy metals for decades. Recovering it protects water quality, wildlife, and the recreation millions depend on.

Impact Statement

Every piece recovered from a lake is one less piece heading downstream to rivers and oceans. Lake cleanups also produce data that guides prevention — from shoreline signage to policy.
