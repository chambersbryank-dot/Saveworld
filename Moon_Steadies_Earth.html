The Moon Steadies Earth’s Tilt

Saving moons, saving planets: the Moon keeps Earth's 23.5-degree tilt in check, which keeps our seasons and climate stable (NASA).

Image: NASA/NOAA DSCOVR EPIC.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 1 source cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • What is it? A natural stabilizer: the Moon keeps Earth’s 23.5-degree tilt in check and sets the rhythm of the tides.
  • Why does it matter? Without it, Earth could wobble more violently on its axis, drastically altering the climate.
  • How does it help people? The Moon sets the highs and lows of our tides, which shape how people use the ocean for food, travel and recreation.

The Five W's of The Moon Steadies Earth’s Tilt

Who

Our Moon, as explained by NASA.

Source: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon

What

A natural stabilizer: the Moon keeps Earth’s 23.5-degree tilt in check and sets the rhythm of the tides.

Source: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon

When

All the time, while Earth slowly pushes the Moon away about 1.5 inches (3.78 cm) a year.

Source: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon

Where

About a quarter-million miles away, in The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary around Earth.

Source: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon

Why

Without it, Earth could wobble more violently on its axis, drastically altering the climate.

Source: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon

Impact Statement

Natural solution

NASA explains that the 23.5-degree tilt keeps Earth safe to live on, because a more exaggerated tilt would cause more extreme seasons. Precisely measuring the Moon’s mass, size and orbit is essential for predicting tides and seasons.

Source: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon

For Further Information: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon

Earth's 23.5-degree tilt is kept in check by the Moon; without it, Earth could wobble more violently, drastically altering the climate.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

The Moon sets the highs and lows of our tides, which shape how people use the ocean for food, travel and recreation.

Source: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon

For Nature

By keeping Earth’s tilt steady, the Moon maintains climate stability and keeps seasons from swinging to extremes.

Source: NASA, 10 things we learn about Earth from the Moon
