ISS Water Recovery: 98%

The space station now recycles 98% of the water its crews bring along, thanks to a Brine Processor Assembly, technology that matters wherever water is scarce.

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

The Urine Processor Assembly recovers water from urine by vacuum distillation; the Brine Processor Assembly then runs the leftover brine through a membrane and evaporates the rest. NASA says the system now reaches its 98% water recovery goal, and the reclaimed water is cleaner than what we drink on Earth.

Source: NASA, water recovery milestone on the ISS

For Further Information: NASA, water recovery milestone on the ISS

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Crews drink water that has been reclaimed, filtered and cleaned so it is cleaner than tap water on Earth, and the same closed-loop thinking is what future crews will need far from home.

Source: NASA, water recovery milestone on the ISS

For Nature

Recovering 98% means only about 2% of the water is lost; the rest keeps going around and around. That is the most efficient water loop people have built.

Source: NASA, water recovery milestone on the ISS
