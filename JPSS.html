Joint Polar Satellite System (NOAA)

NOAA's polar weather satellites circle Earth 14 times a day; polar-orbiting satellites supply about 85% of the data used by weather prediction models (NOAA).

Image: NASA/Liz Wilk (KSC-20221109-PH-LNW01_0001).

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 4 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • JPSS satellites fly over the North and South Poles, around and around.
  • They scan the whole Earth twice a day for temperature, clouds and rain.
  • Forecasters use the data to warn people before hurricanes and blizzards.

The Five W's of JPSS

Who

NOAA, with NASA. The fleet includes Suomi NPP, NOAA-20 (JPSS-1) and NOAA-21 (JPSS-2), with JPSS-3 and JPSS-4 to come.

Source: NOAA NESDIS, Joint Polar Satellite System

What

Satellites that measure atmospheric temperature and moisture, wind shear, land and sea surface temperature, clouds and rainfall rates.

Source: NOAA NESDIS, NOAA satellites have you covered this hurricane season

When

Flying now. JPSS-4 is the next to launch, with a launch readiness date of 2027.

Source: NOAA NESDIS, Joint Polar Satellite System

Where

Pole-to-pole orbits, 14 times a day, giving full global coverage twice daily.

Source: NOAA NESDIS, Joint Polar Satellite System

Why

They provide the majority of data that informs numerical weather forecasting in the U.S.

Source: NOAA NESDIS, Joint Polar Satellite System

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

Data from JPSS and other polar-orbiting satellites provide about 85% of the data used by weather prediction models (NOAA).

Source: NOAA NESDIS, NOAA satellites have you covered this hurricane season

For Further Information: NOAA NESDIS, Joint Polar Satellite System

JPSS satellites The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary pole to pole 14 times a day, covering the whole globe twice daily (NOAA).

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

JPSS data help guide the preparation and evacuation information communities need well before a hurricane strikes (NOAA).

Source: NOAA NESDIS, NOAA satellites have you covered this hurricane season

For Nature

JPSS measures land and sea surface temperatures, observations that also track the condition of oceans and land (NOAA).

Source: NOAA NESDIS, NOAA satellites have you covered this hurricane season
