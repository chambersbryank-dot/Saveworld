Interceptors

The Ocean Cleanup effort to stop river plastic before it reaches the sea.

The Five W's of Interceptors

Who

The Ocean Cleanup, the nonprofit founded by Boyan Slat in 2013 and based in the Netherlands. Interceptors are run by that organization with local partners.

What

Solar-powered river systems — Interceptor Originals, Barriers, Tenders, Guards, and Barricades — that catch plastic in the river and hold it for removal.

When

Announced in October 2019. The first units went into the Klang River in Malaysia and Jakarta, Indonesia. The 30 Cities Program was announced in June 2025 at the UN Ocean Conference.

Where

Deployed rivers in Indonesia, Malaysia, Vietnam, Jamaica, Guatemala, the Dominican Republic, and Los Angeles County, with named 30 Cities work in places such as Panama City, Mumbai, Manila, Bangkok, and Jakarta. Wikipedia listed 26 systems across 12 countries as of 2026; The Ocean Cleanup’s own May 2025 milestone listed 20 Interceptors in 9 countries.

Why

A small number of rivers carry most of the plastic that reaches the ocean. Stopping it in the river is faster than chasing it at sea.

Impact Statement

The 30 Cities Program aims to cut up to one third of river plastic reaching the ocean by 2030 by treating the main polluting waterways in 30 cities, not one river at a time. The first cities named include Jakarta, Montego Bay, Kuala Lumpur, Panama City, Bangkok, Los Angeles, Mumbai, and Manila.
