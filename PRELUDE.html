PRELUDE (ClearSpace)

ClearSpace and ESA rehearse close-proximity rendezvous before debris capture.

  1. Home
  2. Project Rawane
  3. Space Disposal
  4. PRELUDE

Impact Statement

Company solution · on the horizon

Potential solution on the horizon

PRELUDE (with ESA). This is a potential solution ClearSpace is advancing. We expect to know more within the next nine months and will keep you updated here.

ESA and ClearSpace jointly announced PRELUDE in January 2026. Planned for launch in June 2027, it will validate close-proximity rendezvous technology before ClearSpace-1 attempts to capture ESA's retired PROBA-1 satellite.

Source: SpaceNews, ESA and ClearSpace announce PRELUDE (Jan 2026)

Company source: ClearSpace, PRELUDE (company page)

ESA has not published a contract value or funding for PRELUDE, so we count it as on the horizon rather than active.

The Five W's of PRELUDE

Who

ClearSpace, with ESA.

Source: SpaceNews, ESA and ClearSpace announce PRELUDE (Jan 2026)

What

A mission to validate close-proximity rendezvous technology, a step toward capturing debris.

Company source: ClearSpace, PRELUDE (company page)

When

Launch planned for June 2027 (company target).

Company source: ClearSpace, PRELUDE (company page)

Where

Earth orbit, ahead of ClearSpace-1's low Earth orbit removal of PROBA-1.

Why

Getting close to an object safely is the hardest part of removing it. PRELUDE rehearses that step first.
