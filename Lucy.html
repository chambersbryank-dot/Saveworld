Lucy (NASA)

NASA's Lucy is touring asteroids; after Dinkinesh and Donaldjohanson, it reaches its first Trojan asteroid, Eurybates, on 11 August 2027 (NASA).

Image: NASA/KSC.

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Some asteroids travel with Jupiter around the Sun. They are very old.
  • Studying them is like reading fossils of how the planets were made.

The Five W's of Lucy (NASA)

Who

NASA, with Southwest Research Institute leading the science.

Source: NASA Science, Lucy

What

A spacecraft visiting a record number of asteroids, including the Trojans that share Jupiter's The curved path an object takes as it travels around a planet, moon or star, like a satellite circling Earth. Glossary.

Source: NASA Science, Lucy

When

Flybys of Dinkinesh (2023) and Donaldjohanson (2025); Eurybates on 11 August 2027.

Source: NASA Science, Lucy

Where

The main asteroid belt and the Trojan asteroids near Jupiter.

Source: NASA Science, Lucy

Why

Trojan asteroids are leftovers from the formation of the outer planets.

Source: NASA Science, Lucy

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

Lucy's main-belt flybys tested its tracking and imaging before the Trojan science phase begins with Eurybates and its small moon Queta.

Source: NASA Science, Lucy

For Further Information: NASA Science, Lucy

NASA's Lucy will visit Trojan asteroids, leftovers from when the planets formed.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Knowing the shapes and makeup of many kinds of asteroids helps scientists plan how to respond to any that could threaten Earth.

Source: NASA Science, Lucy

For Nature

These ancient rocks preserve clues to how our Solar System, and the materials for life, came together.

Source: NASA Science, Lucy
