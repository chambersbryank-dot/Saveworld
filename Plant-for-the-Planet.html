Plant-for-the-Planet

Started by a 9-year-old in 2007, Plant-for-the-Planet trains young Climate Justice Ambassadors and restores forests, including Mexico's Mayan forests (Plant-for-the-Planet).

Image: SI Partner.

  1. Home
  2. Forests
  3. Plant-for-the-Planet

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 2 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • A 9-year-old boy named Felix asked kids to plant a million trees in every country.
  • Now more than 100,000 young people have learned to help forests and climate.
  • The group has planted over 12 million trees in Mexico's Mayan forests.

The Five W's of Plant-for-the-Planet

Who

Plant-for-the-Planet, a youth-founded nonprofit. At age 9, Felix Finkbeiner called on children to plant one million trees in every country.

Source: Plant-for-the-Planet, About Us

What

Climate education for children and youth, plus forest restoration and a free platform for funding and monitoring restoration projects.

Source: Plant-for-the-Planet

When

Plant-for-the-Planet Germany was founded in 2007.

Source: Plant-for-the-Planet

Where

Worldwide, including the Mayan forests of Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula and restoration sites in Spain.

Source: Plant-for-the-Planet

Why

To fight the climate crisis and The variety of all living things in a place: plants, animals, fungi and tiny microbes. Glossary loss by growing and protecting forests and empowering young people.

Source: Plant-for-the-Planet

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

In the Yucatán, Plant-for-the-Planet has planted over 12 million trees, helps patrol two reserves and is working toward a jaguar corridor (organization figures).

Source: Plant-for-the-Planet

For Further Information: Plant-for-the-Planet, About Us

More than 100,000 children and youth in 76 countries have become Climate Justice Ambassadors.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Its academies teach children and youth to give speeches, plant trees and lead their communities on climate (Plant-for-the-Planet).

Source: Plant-for-the-Planet

For Nature

Restoring Mayan forests and working toward a jaguar corridor protects wildlife habitat (Plant-for-the-Planet).

Source: Plant-for-the-Planet
