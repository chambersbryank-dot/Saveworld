Space

Three solutions under the Space card.

Space solutions

Orbital Data Centers

AI chips on satellites using unlimited solar power and radiative cooling, with no water-based cooling towers. Starcloud launched the first NVIDIA H100 GPU into orbit in November 2025, and Google's Project Suncatcher flew four TPUs on October 1, 2026.

This page covers orbital data centers — see the details above.

Astroscale

The global leader in on-orbit servicing, founded in 2013. Their ADRAS-J mission with JAXA demonstrated rendezvous with a tumbling rocket body, and ADRAS-J2 is contracted for launch to remove debris. They also provide life extension, refueling, and inspection services.

Read the full Astroscale page

ClearSpace

A Swiss in-orbit servicing company leading Europe's first active debris removal mission, ClearSpace-1, commissioned by ESA to capture and deorbit a spent rocket adapter. Their PRELUDE mission validates advanced rendezvous tech ahead of a 2027 launch.
