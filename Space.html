Computing Above the Clouds

Solar-powered data centers in orbit, zero water cooling, and a new frontier for sustainable tech.

The Five W's of Space Data Centers

Who

Starcloud launched the first NVIDIA H100 GPU into orbit in November 2025. Google's Project Suncatcher put four Tensor Processing Units in space on October 1, 2026.

What

AI chips on satellites for cloud computing in orbit. Unlimited solar power and radiative cooling, with no water-based cooling towers.

When

Starcloud-1 launched November 2, 2025. Starcloud-2 is planned for 2027. Google's Suncatcher test flew October 1, 2026.

Where

Low Earth orbit today. Lunar orbit is next, targeted no earlier than 2028.

Why

U.S. data centers use about 17 billion gallons of water a year directly, and far more through electricity. Orbital compute uses sunlight and radiates heat, with no water.

Impact Statement

Space-based data centers offer continuous sunlight and no evaporative cooling. Launch costs still need to fall before orbital compute can compete at scale.
