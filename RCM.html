RADARSAT Constellation Mission (Canada)

Three Canadian radar satellites watch Canada's land, coasts and Arctic day and night, through cloud and smoke; about 250,000 images a year for the Government of Canada (Canadian Space Agency).

Image: NASA (ISS070-E-086805, Feb. 3, 2024).

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • RCM is three satellites that use A tool that sends out radio waves and listens for the echoes. From space it can see the ground through clouds, day or night. Glossary, like a bat uses sound.
  • Radar works in the dark and sees through clouds and smoke.
  • It helps ships find safe paths through ice and helps after floods.

The Five W's of RCM

Who

The Canadian Space Agency plans and operates RCM. Natural Resources Canada runs the ground stations and the data archive.

Source: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ

What

Three identical C-band A radar on a moving satellite that combines many echoes into one sharp picture, as if it had a giant antenna. Glossary satellites. Each carries a ship-tracking (AIS) receiver.

Source: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ

When

Launched on a Falcon 9 from California on June 12, 2019, and declared operational on November 14, 2019.

Source: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ

Where

Daily coverage of Canada's land and waters, the Arctic up to four times a day, and daily access to 90% of the world's surface.

Source: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ

Why

Radar sees through clouds, haze and smoke, so it can map sea ice, floods and burn scars in any weather.

Source: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

The Government of Canada expects to use about 250,000 RCM images a year, 50 times more than the first RADARSAT (Canadian Space Agency).

Source: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ

For Further Information: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ

In ship detection mode, RCM can detect ships 25 m long across all waters within 2,000 km of Canada's coasts, every day (Canadian Space Agency).

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Precise sea ice maps help ships navigate safely, and radar data help northern communities plan buildings and runways on shifting permafrost (Canadian Space Agency).

Source: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ

For Nature

RCM data help monitor A community of living things and the place they live, like a reef or a forest, all depending on each other. Glossary and climate change, and help farmers avoid wasting fertilizer, pesticides and water (Canadian Space Agency).

Source: Canadian Space Agency, RADARSAT Constellation Mission FAQ
