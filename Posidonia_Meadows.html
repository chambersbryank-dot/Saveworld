Posidonia Seagrass Meadows

The Mediterranean seagrass Posidonia oceanica forms meadows that are thousands of years old and store carbon in the seafloor (CEAB-CSIC).

Image: NASA (iss071e207494).

  1. Home
  2. Ocean
  3. Posidonia Seagrass Meadows

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 2 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • Posidonia is a sea grass that grows under the Mediterranean Sea.
  • Some patches have been growing for more than 2,000 years.
  • They hold carbon in the sand and give baby fish a safe home.

The Five W's of Posidonia Seagrass Meadows

Who

A Flowering plants that grow underwater in shallow seas, forming meadows that shelter animals and store carbon. Glossary, Posidonia oceanica, studied by Spanish scientists at CEAB-CSIC and others.

Source: CEAB-CSIC, The Illetes Posidonia meadow is over 2,000 years old

What

Underwater meadows of a slow-growing plant that spreads by cloning itself.

Source: PLOS ONE, Millenary clones in meadows of Posidonia oceanica

When

The Illetes meadow off Mallorca has been growing for about 2,355 years.

Source: CEAB-CSIC, The Illetes Posidonia meadow is over 2,000 years old

Where

The Mediterranean Sea, including the Balearic Islands of Spain.

Source: PLOS ONE, Millenary clones in meadows of Posidonia oceanica

Why

Meadows trap carbon in the Sand, mud and gravel carried by water that settles on river beds, lake bottoms and beaches. Glossary beneath them and shelter sea life, but they are threatened.

Source: CEAB-CSIC, The Illetes Posidonia meadow is over 2,000 years old

Impact Statement

Natural solution · active

Some Posidonia clones may stretch up to 15 km and be thousands of years old, making them among the oldest living things on Earth.

Source: PLOS ONE, Millenary clones in meadows of Posidonia oceanica

For Further Information: CEAB-CSIC, The Illetes Posidonia meadow is over 2,000 years old

A Posidonia seagrass meadow off Mallorca has been growing for about 2,355 years.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Meadows protect beaches from erosion and give nursery habitat to fish that coastal communities depend on.

Source: CEAB-CSIC, The Illetes Posidonia meadow is over 2,000 years old

For Nature

Over centuries, meadows bury carbon in the seafloor, keeping it out of the air.

Source: CEAB-CSIC, The Illetes Posidonia meadow is over 2,000 years old
