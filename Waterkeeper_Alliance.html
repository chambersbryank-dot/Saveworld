Waterkeeper Alliance

Waterkeeper Alliance unites hundreds of local Waterkeeper groups that patrol and protect more than 6 million square miles of rivers, lakes and coastlines (Waterkeeper Alliance).

Image: NASA, Space Shuttle.

  1. Home

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 3 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • A Waterkeeper is a person or group who watches over a river, lake or coast.
  • They check the water and stop people from polluting it.
  • It all started with fishermen who saved the Hudson River in New York.

The Five W's of Waterkeeper Alliance

Who

Waterkeeper Alliance and hundreds of local Waterkeeper groups across six continents.

Source: Waterkeeper Alliance, About Us

What

Waterkeepers patrol their local waters and defend clean water through citizen science, court cases, policy work, grassroots advocacy and community action.

Source: Waterkeeper Alliance, About Us

When

The movement began in 1966 with working-class fishermen on New York's Hudson River. Waterkeeper Alliance was founded in 1999.

Source: Waterkeeper Alliance, About Us

Where

Six continents. One example is the Mahanadi River Waterkeeper in India.

Source: Waterkeeper Alliance

Why

Clean water faces growing threats from PFAS chemicals, plastic pollution, industrial agriculture, aging infrastructure and climate change.

Source: Waterkeeper Alliance, About Us

Impact Statement

Nonprofit solution · active

Together, Waterkeeper groups patrol and protect more than 6 million square miles of waterways, supporting nearly 2.5 billion people (organization figures).

Source: Waterkeeper Alliance, About Us

For Further Information: Waterkeeper Alliance, About Us

Waterkeeper Alliance traces back to a group of fishermen on New York's Hudson River in 1966 whose activism helped the river recover.

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

Waterkeepers defend clean water as a basic right, in waters that support nearly 2.5 billion people (organization figure).

Source: Waterkeeper Alliance, About Us

For Nature

Their goal is water that is clean, healthy and safe for all people and nature to flourish, from rivers and lakes to coastlines.

Source: Waterkeeper Alliance, About Us
