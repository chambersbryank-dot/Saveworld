Global Flood Awareness System (GloFAS)

Free global river flood forecasts up to 30 days ahead, plus 4-month outlooks, from the Copernicus Emergency Management Service (CEMS).

Image: NASA Earth Observatory (ASTER on Terra, Aug. 4, 2010).

  1. Home
  2. Rivers
  3. Global Flood Awareness System (GloFAS)

Last reviewed: October 2026 · 4 sources cited

💡 Explain Like I'm 10

  • GloFAS predicts when rivers might flood, up to a month ahead.
  • It uses weather forecasts and computer models of rivers.
  • Early warnings give families time to move to safety.

The Five W's of GloFAS

Who

The Copernicus Emergency Management Service (CEMS), part of the EU's Copernicus programme.

Source: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)

What

Free and open hydrological predictions: river flood forecasts up to 30 days ahead and outlooks up to 4 months.

Source: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)

When

Running now; a March 2024 report counted 5,373 registered users.

Source: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)

Where

Worldwide, giving a global overview that adds to national and regional forecasts.

Source: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)

Why

A global overview of floods helps disaster management when international aid is needed.

Source: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)

Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

GloFAS gives free flood forecasts up to 30 days ahead to 5,373 registered users, and supports the EU's Emergency Response Coordination Centre (CEMS, March 2024).

Source: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)

For Further Information: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)

GloFAS river forecasts reach up to 30 days ahead, with seasonal outlooks up to 4 months (CEMS).

How This Saves People & the Planet

For People

GloFAS gives national and regional authorities extra information to prepare for floods (CEMS).

Source: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)

For Nature

GloFAS analyses compare river discharge and flood events worldwide, a picture of how rivers are behaving (CEMS).

Source: CEMS Floods, Monitoring and reporting floods worldwide (March 2024)
