Our Mission

Saving a planet is saving people. Saving Planets shares real, working solutions from Earth and space, sourced to the agencies and organizations doing the work.

About Saving Planets

Saving Planets (savingplanets.com) is an independent website that tracks practical fixes for our planet: ocean, lake and river cleanups, forest restoration, and the satellites and missions that watch over Earth and protect it from space hazards. Every figure on the site is sourced to NASA, ESA, JAXA, ISRO or NOAA, or to the organization doing the work and labeled as its own figure. Solutions that aren't funded or confirmed yet are clearly marked as on the horizon. The site is published in English, French, Spanish and Arabic, and takes no donations: every Donate button goes straight to the organization.
