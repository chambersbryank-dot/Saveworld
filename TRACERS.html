Impact Statement

Agency solution · active

NASA's TRACERS (Tandem Reconnection and Cusp Electrodynamics Reconnaissance Satellites) launched on July 23, 2025. The twin satellites fly in low Earth orbit through the polar cusps, funnel-shaped openings in Earth's magnetic field, to study magnetic reconnection: what happens when activity from the Sun meets the Earth's invisible magnetic shield. It steers most of the Sun's charged particles away from our planet. Glossary that protects us. Understanding it helps scientists prepare for the effects of solar activity on Earth.

Source: NASA, TRACERS
